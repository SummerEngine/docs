**Class:** `SummerServiceError` · **Inherits:** RefCounted

Immutable versioned failure identity shared by Summer services.

A service owns its [`family`](/api-reference/gdscript/types/service-error#family), [`family_version`](/api-reference/gdscript/types/service-error#family_version), and stable [`reason`](/api-reference/gdscript/types/service-error#reason). Generic game flows can switch on [`category`](/api-reference/gdscript/types/service-error#category) and [`retry_disposition`](/api-reference/gdscript/types/service-error#retry_disposition) without depending on product routes or one ever-growing global error-code list. Instances are constructed only by trusted native adapters and cannot be mutated or forged by scripts.
