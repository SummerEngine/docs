**Class:** `SummerMatchDeliveryCompletionOperation` · **Inherits:** [`SummerOperation`](/api-reference/gdscript/types/operation)

Typed completion of one persistent World's Match delivery phase.

Finalizes the current Match participants after required delivery becomes terminal without retiring a persistent or multi-Match World. Exact retries are idempotent and expose the same immutable Match and settlement correlation values.
