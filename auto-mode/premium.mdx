MAX lets Summer pick frontier models and experts for demanding tasks. It appears
as **MAX Mode** at the top of the model selector, next to Auto.

MAX is on every paid plan. On the Free plan it shows a lock, and picking it opens
the upgrade prompt.

<Warning>
MAX costs much more than Auto. Watch your usage while you use it.
</Warning>

## Use MAX for

- Long agentic tasks that span many files.
- Complex refactors and architecture work.
- Hard problems where quality matters more than speed or cost.

For everyday work, [Auto](/auto-mode/auto) is faster and uses less of your balance.

## Pick a specific model instead

The models behind MAX can change as new frontier models ship. If you want one
specific model, pick it in the selector. See [Models](/auto-mode/models).
