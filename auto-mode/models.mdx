Summer gives you three ways to choose which model does the work:

- **[Auto](/auto-mode/auto)**: Summer picks for you. The default for new chats, on every plan.
- **[MAX](/auto-mode/premium)**: Summer picks frontier models for demanding tasks. Paid plans only.
- **A specific model**: you pick one yourself in the model selector.

## The selector is the list

The model selector in the chat composer is the source of truth for what you can
pick right now. It is built from the live model catalog. Auto and MAX sit at the
top, then the specific models. Search the list by name, and hover a model to see
its description and context window.

Models are added and retired often. Any model named on this page is a snapshot;
the selector is current.

## What each plan can pick

| | What you can select |
| --- | --- |
| **Signed out** | Auto, plus one OpenAI model (currently Luna 6) |
| **Free plan** | Auto, plus a small set of OpenAI and DeepSeek models (currently Luna 6 and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash) |
| **Paid plans** | Auto, MAX, and every model in the selector |

The specific free models are there on purpose, for people who would rather name
their model than leave the choice to Auto.

Models your plan does not cover still appear in the selector, muted and marked
with a lock. Picking one opens the upgrade prompt and does not switch your chat.

## Providers in the selector

On a paid plan you can pick models from OpenAI, Anthropic and DeepSeek directly,
including a coding-specialist OpenAI model. Auto and MAX can also route to other
providers behind the scenes. The
[subprocessors list](https://www.summerengine.com/legal/subprocessors) names every
provider that can receive your prompts.

## Cost warnings

Some frontier models use your balance much faster than others. They carry a
**Higher cost** marker in the selector, and an estimate of how many messages your
balance has left at that model's rate.

<Info>
If you do not want to think about any of this, leave it on Auto. It is the
default for a reason, and it is what we recommend for most work.
</Info>
