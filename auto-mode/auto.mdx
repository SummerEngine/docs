Auto lets Summer pick the model and experts for your task, so you can stay focused
on the game. It is the default for new chats, it is available on every plan
including Free, and it is what we recommend for most work.

Auto sits at the top of the model selector in the chat composer, next to MAX.

## Use Auto for

- Day-to-day game development: gameplay code, UI, scenes and small refactors.
- Questions about your project.
- Quick edits, prototypes and exploration.

## When to switch

- **[MAX](/auto-mode/premium)** for the hardest work, on paid plans.
- **A specific model** if you want to name the model yourself. See
  [Models](/auto-mode/models).

<Info>
The model behind Auto can change as we improve quality and reliability. If you
need one specific model, pick it in the selector.
</Info>
