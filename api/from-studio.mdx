You are signed in to Summer Studio. Everything your agent needs for the Summer API is one click away, in the same account: the same credits, the same library, the same sign-in.

## Open the Developers hub

In Studio, open **Developers**{/* DEVHUB_ROUTE */}. It has:

- **Connect your agent**: the one command for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or Hermes, and the connector address for ChatGPT and Claude.
- **Models and prices**: every model with its price, the same list as [models and pricing](/api/models).
- **Quickstart**: your first asset in three steps.

You can also ask **Ask Summer**, the chat in Studio: "How do I let my coding agent make assets?" It opens the hub and shows the models and prices.

## Where things are in Studio

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Your agents and apps, each with its spend limit and **Revoke** | [Settings > Connected apps](https://summerengine.com/settings/connected-apps) |
| Credits and top-ups | [Credits](https://summerengine.com/studio/credits) |
| Everything your agent made | Your Summer library in Studio, next to what you made in Studio |

## What it costs

Every model has one public price: the provider's list price. No markup on assets. Your agent prices each job for free before it spends (`summer api estimate`, or [`POST /v1/estimate`](/api/jobs#know-the-price-first)), and jobs use your paid Summer credits: your plan's credits and your top-ups.
