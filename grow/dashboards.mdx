<Note>
  **Early access.** Dashboards are saved in your browser only for now: they are not shared with your team or other devices. Until you have numbers on Summer Games, this page shows example data, marked **Example data**.
</Note>

Open **Dashboards** at [summerengine.com/studio/grow/dashboards](https://summerengine.com/studio/grow/dashboards).

## Make a board

1. Under **New dashboard**, type a name (up to 40 characters) and click **Create**. A new board starts with three tiles: play sessions in the last 7 days, play sessions by day over 28 days, and store page views by day over 28 days.
2. Switch boards with the tabs at the top. **Delete** removes the open board.

## Add a tile

| Choice | Options |
|---|---|
| **Number** | Store page views, launches, play sessions, play time, or one custom event your game sends. |
| **Games** | All your games, or one game. A custom event tile shows one game. |
| **Chart** | Line, bars or a single number. |
| **Window** | 7, 14 or 28 days. |

Click **Add tile**. A board holds up to 24 tiles. Move a tile earlier or later, or remove it, from its own buttons. Tiles draw what Summer Games counted for your games.

## With your agent

Ask "make me a dashboard called Morning check". The Studio assistant opens Dashboards, fills the name and creates it. See [Use the agent](/grow/use-the-agent).
