<Note>
  **Early access.** Store page tests for art, text and language are rolling out; if they are not open for your game yet, the page says so. Price tests and gameplay tests are not open yet. Until you have a game on Summer Games, this page shows example data, marked **Example data**.
</Note>

Open **A/B tests** at [summerengine.com/studio/grow/ab-tests](https://summerengine.com/studio/grow/ab-tests).

## Make a test

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Under **New A/B test**, give it a **Name** (up to 80 characters), such as "Darker key art". Pick the **Game** if you have more than one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what changes">
    **Art** (key art, wide capsule or tall capsule, from your published images), **Text** (game name, tagline, description) or **Language**. Empty text fields keep the published text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add versions">
    Version **A** is your published store page. Add up to three more with **Add version**. Give each a share of visitors; the shares add up to 100%.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose countries (optional)">
    Two-letter codes, such as `DE, AT, CH`. Empty means everyone. A language test suggests countries for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start it">
    Click **Start test**, or **Save as draft** and start it later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Visitors keep their version on every visit. One test runs per game at a time.

## Read the results

Each test shows, per version: visitors, purchases, conversion, revenue and revenue per visitor, and how it compares with A. A difference is marked **clear** only when it is unlikely to be chance; until then it says **Not clear yet**. Give each version a few hundred visitors before you decide.

## Stop it and keep the winner

Click **Stop**, pick the winner (or **No winner**), and confirm. Stopping does not change your store page: to keep the winning art or text, publish it on the game's store page.

## Apply for a test run

Below your tests, **Apply for an A/B test** asks the Summer team to run a test with you, for things you cannot test yourself yet. It is an application: the team reaches out by email, and nothing is charged when you apply.

## With your agent

Ask "test a darker key art against my current one". The Studio assistant creates the test as a draft, and starts or stops it only when you ask. It reads the results and says when a difference is clear. From another agent, use `summer_grow_ab_tests` (`list`, `results`, `create`, `start`, `stop`). See [Use the agent](/grow/use-the-agent).
