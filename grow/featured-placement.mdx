<Note>
  **Early access.** Featured placement is an application: the Summer team decides and books each place with you. Until you have a game on Summer Games, this page shows example data, marked **Example data**.
</Note>

Open **Featured placement** at [summerengine.com/studio/grow/featured-placement](https://summerengine.com/studio/grow/featured-placement).

## Check your readiness

Each game gets a card with how many of seven checks it passes. A ready store page helps your application.

- The store page is published.
- It has a tagline.
- It has a description.
- It has at least 3 tags.
- It has an icon.
- It has at least 4 screenshots.
- People played it in the last 28 days.

**Edit store page** opens the game in Your games to fix what is missing. See [Store page text](/publishing/store-text) and [Store art](/publishing/store-art).

## Apply for a featured place

| Field | What to put |
|---|---|
| **Game** | Which game. Drafts are marked. |
| **Placement** | **Home hero** (the large slot at the top of Summer Games, one game a week), **New and noteworthy** (recent games players are finishing), **Seasonal collection** (holidays and events) or **Genre shelf** (the top of a genre page). |
| **Pitch** | 40 to 400 characters: what makes it special, who it is for, and what is new. |
| **When** | Next available, in two weeks, next month or next season. |
| **What do you hope to achieve?** | Your goal. |
| **Budget in mind** | What you would spend, and the currency. |

Click **Send application**. The Summer team looks at your store page and players, then reaches out by email. Nothing is charged until you agree on a week. An application is not a promise of a place.

## With your agent

Ask "what's missing before my game can be featured?" and the Studio assistant reads the readiness checks and helps you fix them. Ask it to apply and it sends the application with your pitch. From another agent, use `summer_grow_overview` with `include: ["readiness"]` and `summer_grow_apply` with `service: "featured_placement"`. See [Use the agent](/grow/use-the-agent).
