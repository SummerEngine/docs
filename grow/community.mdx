<Note>
  **Early access.** Community shows counts for now. You answer comments on summer.games, not here. Until players wishlist or follow your games, this page shows example data, marked **Example data**.
</Note>

Open **Community** at [summerengine.com/studio/grow/community](https://summerengine.com/studio/grow/community).

## What you see

- **Wishlists** and **follows** across all your games, all time.
- A table of wishlists and follows by game, with likes and comments when Summer Games reports them.
- A **Store page** link for each game.

Summer Games has no star ratings.

For what was added each day and a game's newest comments, open the game in **Games**. See [What is Summer Grow?](/grow/overview#games)

## Grow your community

Wishlists and follows rise when more players find your page. Share your summer.games link with campaign tags and see what each post brings in [Analytics](/grow/analytics#tag-your-links). For more reach, see [Promotions](/grow/promotions), [Featured placement](/grow/featured-placement) and [Paid growth](/grow/paid-growth).

## With your agent

Ask "how many wishlists does my game have?". The Studio assistant reads the same counts. From another agent, use `summer_grow_overview` with `include: ["interest"]`. See [Use the agent](/grow/use-the-agent).
