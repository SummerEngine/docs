The **Summer Games Jam** is a weekly game jam. Each jam has a theme and a closing time. You build a game in a week, upload it in Studio, and your entry shows on the jam's page on summer.games (`summer.games/jams/<jam>`).

The current jam, its theme and its closing time are on [summerengine.com/jam](https://summerengine.com/jam). The rules are on [summer.games/events](https://summer.games/events).

## Enter

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the upload with the jam picked">
    Click **Submit your game** on [summerengine.com/jam](https://summerengine.com/jam), or open `https://www.summerengine.com/studio/upload?event=<jam>`. The link picks the jam for you. You can also open [Publish a game](https://www.summerengine.com/studio/upload) and choose the jam under **Enter a jam (optional)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your game and pick its targets">
    The form shows the theme and when entries close. Choose **Web** to give your entry a Play button in the browser. Click **Save and continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your build">
    In **Check and publish**, upload a web export in the **Web** box (a `.zip` with `index.html` at its root; Godot's standard Web export works, see [web exports](/grow/publish#web-exports)). Add your art and text like any store page. See [Publish your game](/grow/publish).
  </Step>
</Steps>

By submitting you agree to the Summer Games Jam rules and the [Summer Games Terms](https://summer.games/terms).

## Your entry

- **Your entry is your game.** Its title, art and builds on the jam page update as you change them in Studio.
- Every step of your game's page shows **You're in** the jam, with a link to the jam page.
- Until a web export is ready, your entry shows on the jam page without a Play button.
- Your game's **Full dashboard** has a **Game jam** section: enter a jam later, or **Withdraw**.

A jam entry does not publish the game. To list it on Summer Games itself, click **Publish** on its store page as usual.

## With your agent

Ask your agent to "enter my game in the jam". Through the Summer Engine MCP it lists the open jams with `jams.list` and enters with `jams.enter`. Entering always asks you: the answer is a link where you agree to the jam rules. `jams.withdraw` takes the entry off. In Studio, the assistant picks the jam on **Publish a game** for you. See [Use the agent](/grow/use-the-agent).
