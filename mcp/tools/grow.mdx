### summer_grow_analytics

On the hosted MCP.

One game's analytics on Summer Games for the last 7, 28 or 90 days (UTC): totals (store page views, launches, play sessions, play time, average session), every day of the window, and the game's top custom events (sent with Summer.client.analytics.capture; counts are approximate). Real data; read-only. gameId comes from [`summer_store_list_games`](/mcp/tools/publish#summer_store_list_games).
