### summer_grow_ab_tests

On the hosted MCP.

The signed-in owner's store A/B tests on Summer Games, through the same owner-scoped proxy as Studio &gt; Grow &gt; A/B tests. action list: the game's tests; results: visitors, purchases, conversion and revenue per version; create: a draft with 2-4 versions (version A first, usually \{\} = the published store page; others change name, subtitle, description, locale and/or art asset ids from the game's published media); start; stop (optional winner letter: a winning price becomes the game's price; winning text or art must then be published through the store page). One test runs per game at a time. Price tests are not open yet. Change tests only when the owner asks.
