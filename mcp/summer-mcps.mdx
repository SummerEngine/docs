Summer has two public MCP servers. One is for making games. One is for playing them with friends.

| | **Summer Engine MCP** | **Summer Games MCP** |
|---|---|---|
| For | Creators who build games | Players on [summer.games](https://summer.games) |
| What the AI does | Edits scenes, writes GDScript, imports and generates assets, runs and debugs the game | Reads who of your friends is online, sends messages, invites friends to a Game, tells you when they join, handles rivals, and opens pages in the latest Summer Games desktop app |
| Where it runs | On your computer, next to the Summer Engine editor (`npx summer-engine mcp`) | On Summer's servers: `https://mcp.summer.games/mcp` |
| Works in | Coding agents: Claude Code and [many more](/mcp/agents) | Claude (web, desktop and phone), ChatGPT, Claude Code and any MCP app with OAuth sign-in |
| Sign in | `npx summer-engine login` | Your Summer account, on summer.games |
| Start here | [MCP setup](/mcp/setup) | [Play with friends from Claude or ChatGPT](/mcp/summer-games) |
| Every tool | [Tools reference](/mcp/tools-reference) | [Summer Games MCP tools](/mcp/summer-games-tools) |

You can connect both. Build a game with the Summer Engine MCP, then ask your AI app who of your friends is online and invite them to play. Today you sign in to each one on its own.

## Summer Engine MCP: build games

The Summer Engine MCP gives a coding agent control of the Summer Engine editor. The agent uses the same operations as the chat inside Summer Engine. One command sets it up for your agent:

```bash
npx -y summer-engine@latest setup <agent> --yes
```

## Summer Games MCP: play with friends

The Summer Games MCP lets an AI app act on your Summer account, as you. Ask it "who of my friends is online?", "text my friends and ask who wants to play" or "invite them to Star Weavers", and it does it. Claude and ChatGPT show the answers as cards in the chat, and tell you when a friend joins. You sign in on summer.games and choose what it may do. You can disconnect it at any time.

## How they differ

| | Summer Engine MCP | Summer Games MCP |
|---|---|---|
| Account | Your Summer Engine account | Your Summer account on summer.games |
| Permissions | Everything the editor can do in the open project | Two: **read** (see your friends, messages, party, inbox, rivals) and **act** (send, invite, change your profile and settings, open desktop pages). You choose on summer.games. |
| Before it changes something | The edit shows in the editor, and undo works | Your AI app asks you before each act tool |
| Costs | Engine control and asset search are free. Cloud generation uses credits. | No tool buys anything or spends Sparks |
| Stop it | Remove the server from your agent's MCP config | Settings → Connected apps on summer.games |

In both, the AI app never sees your password. You sign in on a Summer page.

## Words we use

These words mean the same thing on both servers and in the Summer apps.

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Game** | A game players find and play on Summer Games. |
| **Project** | A creator's project in Summer Engine. A Project can become a Game. |
| **Player** | A person on Summer, seen through their Summer account. A player has a display name and a unique `@handle`. |
| **Friend** | A player who accepted your friend request, or whose request you accepted. Messages go only to friends. |
| **Party** | A group of players that stays together across Games. It has a leader and a party chat. |
| **Invite** | An invitation to join your party. The other player accepts or declines it. |
| **Message** | A direct message to one friend, or a message in your party chat. |
| **Notification** | An entry in your Summer inbox, such as a friend request or a party invite. |
| **Rival** | A player you both agreed to compete with. A rival request works like a friend request. Player against player only. |
| **Connected app** | An AI app you allowed to act for your Summer account. |
| **Sparks** | Summer's currency. No MCP tool buys or spends Sparks. |
