### set_auto_top_up

`account.set_auto_top_up`: **Change auto top-up**

Ask the person to turn auto top-up on or off or change its amounts: when credits fall below one amount, Summer adds another from their saved card, within a monthly limit. Always needs their yes in Studio. Next: jobs.wait.

| | |
|---|---|

| **CLI** | `summer account set-auto-top-up` |

| **Effect** | Changes your own drafts, files or settings. Nobody else sees it yet. |
| **Asks you first** | Always. You approve it in Studio; an agent can never skip this. |

**Inputs:**

| Input | Type | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|---|

| `addUsd` | number | No | How much to add each time, in US dollars, whole cents (5 to 1000). Range 5 to 1000. |
| `monthlyLimitUsd` | number | No | The most added in one calendar month, in US dollars, whole cents (5 to 5000). Range 5 to 5000. |
