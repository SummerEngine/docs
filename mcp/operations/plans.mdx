### answer

`plans.answer`: **Answer a new game's planning question**

Answer a planning question of a game started on the create page, as the person would: its option ids, their own words, or nothing to skip. Enough answers start Summer drawing the plan. Next: plans.show or plans.finish_questions.

| **CLI** | `summer plans answer` |
