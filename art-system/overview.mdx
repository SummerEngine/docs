Summer Studio is the web app at [summerengine.com/studio](https://www.summerengine.com/studio).
For assets, it does three jobs:

- **Create** images, video, audio and 3D models with AI models.
- **Keep** what you make and upload in **My Assets**, sorted into folders.
- **Find** free assets in the [Asset Store](https://www.summerengine.com/asset-store).

Then ask Summer Engine, the game engine built for AI, to put any of these assets
into your game. It imports the file into your project and, for a 3D model, can
place it in the scene.

## What you can make

| Kind | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Image** | Key art, character sheets, item icons, sprites, store capsules, concept art |
| **Video** | Clips from words or from an image, trailers and short game ads |
| **Audio** | Speech, sound effects, music, and speech-to-text |
| **3D** | Models from words, one picture or several views, with optional rigging and animation |
| **Effects** | Game VFX that the agent builds in your scene, and tools that turn one image into game-ready assets |

Each generation uses credits from your balance. The generator shows the cost
before you start. See [Pricing](/essentials/pricing) for plans.

## Getting assets into your game

In the engine chat, ask for what you need, for example "add the knight model I made
in Studio to the main scene" or "find a wooden crate and put it next to the door".
Summer searches your assets and the Asset Store, imports the file and wires it up.

Agents that use the Summer MCP do the same with the `engine.import_asset` and
`engine.import_asset_by_id` operations. See
[MCP asset search](/ai-tools/mcp-asset-search).

## Uploading your own assets

You can upload your own images (PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF) and 3D models (GLB, up to
500 MB) to My Assets. Uploads are private by default.

## Who can see your assets

- **Free plan**: assets you generate are public in the Asset Store.
- **Paid plans**: you choose public or private per asset, and you can set a default
  in your settings. Assets that are private stay private if you later cancel.

Every asset page shows how it was made: **Handmade**, or **Made with** and the
model name for AI-generated assets.
