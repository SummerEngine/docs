The [Summer Asset Store](https://www.summerengine.com/asset-store) is a library of
free game assets: some made by people, some made with AI and shared by Summer users.
You can browse it on the web, inside Summer Studio under **Public Assets**, or ask
the agent to search it for you.

## Browse and search

- **Categories**: 3D Models, Sprites, 2D Art, Music, Sound Effects and Animations.
- **Collections**: themed groups such as sword sprites, crates and barrels, castle
  assets, or gems and crystals.
- **Search**: type what you need, for example "medieval sword" or "pixel art
  character". Specific words work better than general ones.

## The asset page

Each asset page shows a preview, the title, how the asset was made, its
[license](/art-system/licensing) and the date. You can:

- **Download** the file.
- **Copy image** or **Share** a link.
- React with a heart, thumbs up or fire, and leave a comment.

How the asset was made appears at the top: **Handmade** for assets made by people,
or **Made with** and the model name for AI-generated assets.

## Import an asset into your game

You do not need to download by hand. In the engine chat, ask Summer for the asset,
for example "import the wooden crate from the asset store into my level". Summer
finds it, imports it into your project, and for a 3D model can place it in the
scene.

MCP agents use `engine.import_asset` (search and import the best match) or
`engine.import_asset_by_id` (one exact asset). See
[MCP asset search](/ai-tools/mcp-asset-search).

To keep a copy in your own library, open the asset under **Public Assets** in
Studio and press **Add to My Assets**. Someone else's asset becomes a private copy
there.

## Share your own assets

Make one of your assets public from its Studio page, and it appears in the Asset
Store for everyone. On the Free plan, assets you generate are public by default.

Selling assets is not open yet. Before it opens, the fees will be published on the
[marketplace values page](https://www.summerengine.com/asset-store/values).

## Report a problem

If an asset breaks the rules or uses someone else's work without permission, tell
us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/summerengine) or at
[support@summerengine.com](mailto:support@summerengine.com), with the asset link.
