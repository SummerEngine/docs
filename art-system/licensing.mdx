Every asset in Summer Studio and the [Asset Store](/art-system/gallery) carries one
license. The asset page shows it next to the title.

| License | What you can do | What you must do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CC0** (default) | Use, share and remix the asset for any purpose, including commercial games | Nothing. No credit needed |
| **CC BY 4.0** | Use, share and remix the asset for any purpose, including commercial games | Credit the original creator and Summer |
| **Private** | Only the owner can see and use the asset | Ask the owner before you use it |

Asset Store assets marked **Free** follow CC0.

## Crediting a CC BY 4.0 asset

Put the credit where players can find it, such as your game's credits screen, and
in your store page or README:

```
"Stone Texture Pack" by CreatorName, from the Summer Asset Store, licensed CC BY 4.0
```

Name the asset, the creator, Summer, and the license. If you change the asset, say
so: "modified from ...".

## Your own assets

You keep ownership of the content you create with Summer Engine. See the
[Terms of Service](https://www.summerengine.com/terms).

When you make one of your assets public, other people can use it under the license
shown on its page.

## Questions and disputes

If you think an asset in the store uses your work without permission, tell us on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/summerengine), or send the asset link to
[support@summerengine.com](mailto:support@summerengine.com).

Summer cannot give legal advice. For complex licensing questions, ask a lawyer.
