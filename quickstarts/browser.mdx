Open the [Summer Engine download page](https://www.summerengine.com/download). On a PC running Windows 10 or newer, choose **Download for Windows**. On a Mac with Apple silicon, choose **Download for macOS**.

## What else you can do in the browser

In [Summer Studio](https://www.summerengine.com/studio), you can explore the tools for 2D art, 3D models, audio and video before you sign in.
