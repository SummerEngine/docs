Start with nothing installed. You describe your idea on summerengine.com, and Summer plans the game with you: a few questions, then a storyboard of concept pictures. The Summer app on your computer then builds it.

**You need:** a web browser. To build the game in step 4: a Windows 10 or newer PC, or a Mac with Apple silicon.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe your game">
    Go to [summerengine.com](https://www.summerengine.com) and type your idea into the box on the page. For example: "A cozy farming game on a floating island where I trade with sky whales."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer a few questions">
    Summer asks a few quick questions about your game. Answer them, then sign up or sign in to see your storyboard. The account is free.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shape the storyboard">
    Summer draws a board of concept pictures for your game: its world, characters and moments. Pick the ones that fit, or ask for changes in plain words, such as "make it look like a watercolor painting".

    **If everything worked, you see:** your storyboard, saved to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build it in the Summer app">
    Press **Build**. Then [install the Summer app](/quickstarts/fresh-project) and sign in with the same account. Your game is in the sidebar under **Your games**. Click **Build it on this computer**, and Summer starts building from your plan.

    **If everything worked, you see:** the game in the editor next to the chat, with Summer's steps in the chat as it builds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What else you can do in the browser

[Summer Studio](https://www.summerengine.com/studio) runs in your browser and works on the games you build anywhere:

- **Make assets**: 3D models, sprite sheets, tilesets, sound and music. See [Summer Studio](/art-system/overview).
- **Publish**: the store page, the build upload and going live on Summer Games. See [Publish from Summer Studio](/grow/publish).
- **Grow**: players, sessions, earnings and promotions. See [Summer Grow](/grow/overview).
