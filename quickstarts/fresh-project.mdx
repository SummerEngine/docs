In five steps you install Summer, describe your first Summer game, and play it. Summer's agent writes the code, builds the scenes and adds the art while you watch.

<Note>
Prefer Claude Code, Codex or Cursor? Follow [Quickstart: your coding agent](/quickstarts/coding-agent) instead.
</Note>

**You need:** a Windows 10 or newer PC, or a Mac with Apple silicon, and about 3 GB of free space.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Summer">
    Download Summer from [summerengine.com/download](https://www.summerengine.com/download).

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Windows">
        Run the setup file you downloaded.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="macOS">
        Open the download, then open **Install Summer**.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    Setup shows each step as it runs: **Check this computer**, **Download Summer Engine** and **Install the summer command**. When it finds Claude Code, Codex or Cursor on your computer, it also connects them to Summer.

    **If everything worked, you see:** "Summer is ready" and an **Open Summer** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Click **Open Summer**. Your browser opens the Summer sign-in page. Sign in or create a free account, then choose how you want to build. **Start free** works for this quickstart.

    **If everything worked, you see:** "You're signed in" in the Summer window. Summer, the `summer` command and your agents now all use this account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a start">
    Summer opens on **Home**. The first time, it asks how to begin:

    - **Start with the Sun Temple** (recommended): a finished world with lighting, characters and props that you remix into your own game. It downloads about 3.7 GB first.
    - **Start empty**: describe your game and Summer builds it from nothing.

    Not sure what to make? Click **Find an idea together**, and Summer asks you a few quick questions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your game">
    Type what you want to play into **Describe the game you want to make…**. For example:

    ```text
    Make a third-person adventure in a desert ruin. I collect five glowing relics while
    stone guardians patrol. Show how many relics I have, and play a sound when I pick one up.
    ```

    Summer names the game, writes the code, builds the scenes and adds assets. Each step shows in the chat, such as "Adding Relic" and "Playing the game". Summer plays the game and looks at it before it says it is done.

    **If everything worked, you see:** the game in the editor next to the chat, and a result with **Play** and **Fix it**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play it and change it">
    Click **Play** in the top bar (**F5** on Windows, **Cmd+R** on Mac). Then ask for the next change in plain words: "Make the guardians faster", "Add a main menu".

    Every turn can be undone: open the **Changes** tab and click **Undo this turn**, or choose **Rewind to here** on any message. To point at something, press **Shift+D** for Design mode and draw on the game view.
  </Step>
</Steps>
