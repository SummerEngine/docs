In five steps you install Summer, describe your first Summer game, and play it. Summer's agent writes the code, builds the scenes and adds the art while you watch.

<Note>
Prefer Claude Code, Codex or Cursor? Follow [Quickstart: your coding agent](/quickstarts/coding-agent) instead.
</Note>

**You need:** a Windows 10 or newer PC, or a Mac with Apple silicon, and a few GB of free space.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Summer">
    Download Summer from [summerengine.com/download](https://www.summerengine.com/download).

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Windows">
        Run `SummerEngine-<version>-Setup.exe`. If Windows SmartScreen shows a warning, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="macOS">
        Open `Summer.dmg`, move **Summer** into your Applications folder and open it. When macOS asks about an app downloaded from the internet, click **Open**.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    **If everything worked, you see:** Summer Engine opens on Home: **SUMMER ENGINE** and one message box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Your browser opens the Summer sign-in page. Sign in or create a free account, and confirm with **Yes, sign in**. The Summer window continues by itself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a start">
    The first time, before you have any games or chats, Summer offers two starts:

    - **Start with the Sun Temple** (recommended): a finished 3D world with lighting, characters and props that you remix into your own game. It downloads in the background, about 3.7 GB; the card shows its progress, and the game opens when it is ready, on a welcome card: **Make it an adventure game**, **Make it a shooter**, **Make it a horror game** or **Show me what is in this world**. Pick one and Summer starts building.
    - **Start empty**: describe your game and Summer builds it from nothing.

    Not sure what to make? Click **Find an idea together**, and Summer asks you two or three quick questions. Later, **New game** offers a starter game, the Sun Temple, an empty game and templates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your game">
    On **Start empty**, type what you want to play into **Describe the game you want to make…**. In the Sun Temple, type the next change into the chat. For example:

    ```text
    Make a third-person adventure in a desert ruin. I collect five glowing relics while
    stone guardians patrol. Show how many relics I have, and play a sound when I pick one up.
    ```

    Summer names the game, writes the code, builds the scenes and adds assets. Each step shows in the chat, such as "Adding Relic" and "Playing the game". Summer plays the game and looks at it before it says it is done.

    **If everything worked, you see:** the game in the editor next to the chat, and a result with **Play** and **Fix it**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play it and change it">
    Click **Play** in the top bar (**F5** on Windows, **Cmd+R** on Mac). Then ask for the next change in plain words: "Make the guardians faster", "Add a main menu".

    Every turn can be undone: open the **Changes** tab and click **Undo this turn**, or choose **Rewind to here** on any message. To point at something, press **Ctrl+Shift+D** (**Cmd+Shift+D** on Mac) for Design mode and draw on the game view.
  </Step>
</Steps>
