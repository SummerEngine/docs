## Make a PlayStation game with Summer Engine

Summer Engine is the game engine built for AI. Build and release your game on PC first, with Summer Agent or your own coding agent. Summer Engine has no built-in PlayStation export: PlayStation builds need Sony's SDK, which you get only after PlayStation Partners approves you.

## Step 1: Build your game on PC

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make your game in Summer Engine">
    Target Windows or Mac first, and release there: [Summer Games](/grow/publish), [Steam](/publishing/steam) or [itch.io](/publishing/itch-io).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Polish your demo">
    PlayStation Partners wants to see quality gameplay and visuals and a clear market for your game.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply to PlayStation Partners">
    Apply at [partners.playstation.net](https://partners.playstation.net). You need a registered company, a game pitch and a business plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What PlayStation needs

- **PlayStation Partners approval**: required before you get the SDK.
- **An NDA**: required for SDK access.
- **Dev kit hardware**: bought from Sony after approval.
- **A registered company**.

## Port it

Give your PC build to a porting studio that has PlayStation access, or, once you are approved, email the Summer team with proof of approval. See [Console publishing](/publishing/consoles).

<Info>Release on PC first. Use that release to show demand and fund the PlayStation port.</Info>
