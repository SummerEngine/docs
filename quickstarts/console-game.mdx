## Make a console game with Summer Engine

Summer Engine is the game engine built for AI. You build and test your game on Windows or Mac with Summer Agent or your own coding agent. Summer Engine has no built-in export to Nintendo Switch, PlayStation or Xbox: console builds need each platform holder's SDK, which you get only after they approve you. Plan the console version as a port of a finished PC game.

## Step 1: Build your game on PC first

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make and polish your game">
    Build it in Summer Engine and release it on PC first: Windows and Mac, on [Summer Games](/grow/publish), [Steam](/publishing/steam) or [itch.io](/publishing/itch-io).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make a strong demo">
    Platform holders want to see a short, polished slice of gameplay, good visuals and a clear hook.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply to the developer programs">
    Nintendo Developer Portal, PlayStation Partners or ID@Xbox. Each has its own approval process.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What console publishing needs

- **Platform holder approval** from Nintendo, Sony or Microsoft.
- **A registered company** or legal entity, for most programs.
- **Dev kit hardware** and the platform SDK, which you get after approval.
- **NDAs and agreements** with the platform holder.

## Two ways to port

| Route | How it works |
|---|---|
| **A porting studio** | You give them your PC build. They do the console export, optimization and certification. |
| **Do it yourself** | After you are approved and have SDK access, email the Summer team with proof of approval. See [Console publishing](/publishing/consoles). |

<Info>Release on PC first. Most indie games reach consoles as a port after they have players and proven demand.</Info>
