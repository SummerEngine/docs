## Make an iPhone game with Summer Engine

Summer Engine is the game engine built for AI. Build your game with Summer Agent or your own coding agent, then publish it to the Summer Games app on iPhone. Players there run your game inside the app, so you need no export template.

## Step 1: Build your game

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download Summer Engine">
    Download it from [summerengine.com/download](https://summerengine.com/download) for Mac (Apple silicon) or Windows. See [Install Summer Engine](/essentials/installation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a project">
    Start a new project and describe the game you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it work on a phone">
    Ask for it: "Add touch controls", "Make the UI fit phone screens", "Lock the game to portrait".
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2: Turn on mobile textures

iPhone needs ETC2/ASTC textures. In **Project → Project Settings → Rendering → Textures → VRAM Compression**, turn on **Import ETC2 ASTC** and wait for the reimport.

## Step 3: Export and publish

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export a Summer Engine export">
    Ask your agent to export with `summer_export_game` and `targets: ["ios"]`, or open **Project → Export**, pick the **summer.games** preset and click **Export Project**. You get one `.zip`. See [Export your game](/publishing/export).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the store page">
    In Summer Studio, pick **iPhone** as a target. It opens the **Mobile store**: text, art and a phone preview. See [Publish your game](/grow/publish).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload and submit">
    Upload the export in **Check and publish**, then click **Publish**. As soon as the automatic checks pass, players find your game in the Summer Games app on iPhone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your own App Store release

Summer Engine has iPhone export from its upstream base, but Summer's export template download (`summer_export_templates`) has web, Mac and Windows templates today, not iPhone. Summer Games does not take `.ipa` files. If you need your own App Store listing, ask on [Discord](https://discord.gg/summerengine) before you plan around it.
