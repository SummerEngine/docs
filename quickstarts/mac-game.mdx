## Make a Mac game with Summer Engine

Summer Engine is the game engine built for AI. Build your game with Summer Agent or your own coding agent, then export a Mac app. The export is a universal app: it runs on Intel and Apple silicon Macs. The Summer Engine editor itself runs on Apple silicon Macs and Windows.

## Step 1: Build your game

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download Summer Engine">
    Download it from [summerengine.com/download](https://summerengine.com/download). See [Install Summer Engine](/essentials/installation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a project">
    Start a new project and describe the game you want in plain words.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Iterate with AI">
    Refine it in conversation: "Add a settings menu", "Add a fullscreen toggle".
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2: Turn on texture formats

In **Project → Project Settings → Rendering → Textures → VRAM Compression**, turn on **Import ETC2 ASTC** (Apple silicon) and **Import S3TC BPTC** (Intel). A universal app needs both. Wait for the reimport.

## Step 3: Export for macOS

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="With your agent">
    Ask your agent to run `summer_export_game` with `targets: ["macos"]` and `format: "download"`. You get `<name>-macos-universal.zip`: a `.app` in a `.zip`, ad hoc signed. See [Export your game](/publishing/export).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="In the editor">
    Open **Project → Export → Add → macOS**, set the **bundle identifier** to your own ID (for example `com.yourstudio.yourgame`), and click **Export Project**. You get a `.app`.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The Mac app of Summer Engine comes with the Mac export template. On Windows, get it with `summer_export_templates` (`"action": "install", "platforms": ["macos"]`).

## Step 4: Publish

- **Summer Games**: zip the `.app` and upload it in the **macOS** box in Summer Studio. See [Publish your game](/grow/publish).
- **Steam or itch.io**: sign and notarize the app first. See [macOS export](/publishing/macos-export).
