Summer runs a server for every match, brings players together through matchmaking, and keeps their progress. You don't rent or manage any machines. Your agent builds the online part with Summer's multiplayer skills, and you test it with several players on your own computer.

<img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/guide-art/multiplayer-quickstart/hero-light.svg" alt="Eight players joined to one World that Summer runs, surrounded by what Summer provides: servers, matchmaking, parties, saves, leaderboards and a store." />
<img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/guide-art/multiplayer-quickstart/hero-dark.svg" alt="Eight players joined to one World that Summer runs, surrounded by what Summer provides: servers, matchmaking, parties, saves, leaderboards and a store." />

**You need:** a Summer game open in Summer Engine, and an agent: Summer's agent in the [Summer app](/quickstarts/fresh-project), or your [coding agent](/quickstarts/coding-agent) with the Summer skills.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for multiplayer">
    Paste this into Summer's chat or your coding agent. Two words it uses: a **World** is one running match, run by one server, and a **queue** is how players find a World, such as "casual".

    <Prompt description="**Make my game multiplayer.** A client, a server, joining a match, and a first test with two players.">
    Make my Summer game multiplayer. Use the Summer `multiplayer` skill first to explain how it fits my game, then `multiplayer-project` to set it up.

    - Read my project and tell me which parts become the server and which stay on each player's device before you change anything.
    - Set up the client and server scenes, the World and a queue, and joining.
    - Start a Local Play test with two players and tell me what you saw.
    </Prompt>

    Your agent first states its plan. Then it adds a `client/` folder for what players run, an `authority/` folder for the server that owns the rules, a shared `network/` folder, and the files `summer.build.json` and `world.json` that tell Summer how a match starts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play it with two players">
    Your agent starts **Local Play**: the server runs in the background and one game window opens per player, side by side, each signed in as a test player. No account or server is needed.

    To start it yourself while Summer Engine is open:

    ```bash
    npx -y summer-engine@latest engine play --players 2
    ```

    **If everything worked, you see:** two game windows, and each one shows one character per player who joined.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add movement and shared state">
    Ask for the next part in the same chat:

    <Prompt description="**Make movement feel instant and share the world.** Each player's own character responds at once; scores and pickups stay in sync.">
    Use the Summer `multiplayer-movement` skill so each player's own character moves the moment they press a key and everyone else's moves smoothly. Then use `multiplayer-state` for [WHAT PLAYERS SHARE, such as the score and pickups]. Test with Local Play with two players, then again with added lag, and tell me what you saw in each window.
    </Prompt>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish it">
    When it plays well, ask your agent to publish:

    <Prompt description="**Publish my multiplayer game.** The export with its server, the store page, and going live after you say yes.">
    Publish my multiplayer game on Summer Games. Use the Summer `multiplayer-publish` skill for the export with its server, then `publish-your-game` for the store page and the upload. Ask me before it goes live.
    </Prompt>

    Publishing needs a sign-in (`npx -y summer-engine@latest login`). Your agent exports the game with its server, makes the store page and uploads the build. It asks you before the game goes live, and if Summer needs your approval, it gives you a link to click.

    **If everything worked:** your game has its page at `summer.games/games/<your-game>`. Play it online with friends in the Summer Games app from [summer.games/download](https://summer.games/download). Every step: [Publish with your agent](/publishing/agent-publishing).
  </Step>
</Steps>
