Summer's agent lives in the chat next to your game. You describe a change in plain words, and it writes the code, builds the scenes, adds assets, plays the game and looks at it before it tells you what changed. New to Summer? Start with [Quickstart: Summer app](/quickstarts/fresh-project).

## Ask for what you want to play

Describe the result, not the steps. Say what the player does, sees and hears:

```text
When I pick up a relic, play a chime, flash the screen gold, and add 1 to the counter
at the top. After the fifth relic, open the temple door.
```

Then play it and ask for the next change: "Make the door open slower", "The guardians are too easy". Short follow-ups work best, one idea at a time.

Not sure what to make? Click **Find an idea together** on Home, and Summer asks you a few questions first.

## Show it, don't only tell it

- **Pictures**: paste a screenshot or attach images, such as a reference for the look you want or a bug you see.
- **Design mode**: press **Shift+D**, then click to select something in the game view or drag to draw on it. Your drawing goes to the agent with your next message.
- **Voice**: click **Dictate** in the message box and speak for up to 45 seconds. Dictation is part of Summer Pro.

## Choose a model

In the chat, the model picker starts on **Auto**, which picks a good model for each turn. Set it to favour **Balance**, **Intelligence** or **Cost**. You can also pick one model yourself. Premium models ask you before they are used. Free accounts get a number of messages each day.

## Run several chats at once

Open up to six chats, also on the same game. One chat can build a level while another makes the menu. Big tasks can also start their own helpers, which show in the chat as **Subagents**. Chats are kept on your computer and backed up to your account.

## Undo anything

- **Undo this turn**: open the **Changes** tab next to the chat.
- **Rewind to here**: on any message, go back to how the game was at that point.
- **Edit message**: change what you asked and run it again.

The agent writes only inside your game's folder. Your game is plain project files on your computer that you can keep in Git.

## Work the way you like

- **Play** in the top bar runs the game (**F5** on Windows, **Cmd+R** on Mac). The arrow next to it has more: play in its own window, muted, or one scene.
- The workspace tabs **3D**, **2D**, **Script**, **Game** and **Studio** sit next to the chat. **Settings > Layout** switches between Simple and Advanced, or picks a preset such as **Focus chat** or **Full editor**.
- **Settings > Connections** adds tools to the agent, such as Blender in one click.
- **Cmd+K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows) searches commands, chats, games and settings.

## Use your own coding agent too

Claude Code, Codex and Cursor can drive the same game. In **Settings > Connections**, click **Copy prompt** and paste it into your agent. See [Quickstart: coding agent](/quickstarts/coding-agent).
