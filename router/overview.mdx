Summer Router is the AI layer for games made with Summer. A player talks to a guard with their voice, and the guard opens the gate when the player says the password. A shopkeeper sells through a tool call. A narrator speaks a line on demand. The game makes a picture from a prompt.

Fetch any page as Markdown: add `.md` to the URL, for example `https://docs.summerengine.com/router/quickstart.md`.

## How it works

1. **You describe a character in a file.** `ai/characters/guard.json` holds its instructions, voice, tools and limits. See [Characters](/router/characters).
2. **You push it to Summer.** `summer router push` checks the file and checks that your GDScript handles every tool, then saves it on Summer for your game. See [CLI](/router/cli).
3. **The game sends only an id and the player's input.** `Summer.client.router.talk("guard")`. Summer holds the instructions, model, voice and keys. See [GDScript](/router/gdscript).
4. **Summer runs the turn** with the provider, calls your tools on the player's device, meters the usage and enforces your caps.

A build never holds a key, a prompt or a model name. A player cannot turn your guard into a general chatbot, because the build cannot change what the character is unless the file allows it.

## What you can use

| Kind | GDScript | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Text turn with a character (tools allowed) | `Summer.client.router.reply()` or a session | [Character reply](/router/character-reply) |
| Realtime voice with a character (tools allowed) | `Summer.client.router.talk(id, {"mode": "voice"})` or `SummerRouterCharacter` | [Character voice](/router/character-voice) |
| Text to speech in a character's voice | `Summer.client.router.speak()` | [Speech](/router/speech) |
| Audio clip to text | `Summer.client.router.transcribe()` | [Transcribe](/router/transcribe) |
| Image from a prompt | `Summer.client.router.generate("image", ...)` | [Image](/router/image) |

## Who pays, and what players see

Usage is billed from your Summer account; spend is visible in Studio. Players never pay and never see a key. Caps are on by default: per player per day, per game per day and month, and per account per month. See [Limits and usage](/router/limits-and-usage).

Any signed-in Summer player can use your characters inside your game. Realtime voice needs a confirmed adult; other players get text. AI is always optional: every error leaves the game running. See [Errors](/router/errors).

## The microphone

Summer asks for the microphone, never your game, and only when the player taps Talk. A player who says no types instead, and the character still speaks. See [Microphone permissions](/router/permissions).
