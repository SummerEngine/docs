Summer is the game engine built for AI. **Summer Router** puts AI inside the game itself, while people play: a guard who argues with the player and opens the gate when convinced, a narrator who reads the player's name, a portrait painted for the player's character. Your game names a character and sends what the player said. Summer does the rest.

<Note>
**Early preview.** Summer Router works on games published on Summer Games. Coming soon for all platforms and storefronts.
</Note>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your game calls one line">
    `Summer.client.router.reply("guard", "Let me in.")` names a character you made in Summer Studio and sends the player's words.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Summer app adds who is playing">
    The game runs in the Summer app on the player's computer, phone or browser. The app knows the signed-in player and the game, so every call belongs to one real player.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Summer Router runs the character">
    Summer checks your caps, adds the character's instructions, runs the model you chose, and runs the safety checks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The answer comes back to the game">
    The game gets the reply, the tool calls the character made, or the voice line or image. Summer meters the call from your Summer credits.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Nothing secret is in your build.** Keys, instructions, model choice and caps stay on Summer. A player who opens your game's files finds a character id and nothing to steal.

## Built to be safe to ship

- **Caps on from day one.** Every game starts with spend caps per player, per game and per account, and rate limits per player and per game. You raise them when your game grows. See [limits and caps](/router/limits).
- **Real players only.** Every call comes from a signed-in player in the Summer app, so per-player caps hold and a script cannot drain your credits.
- **Your credits, your control.** Calls are paid from your Summer credits, the same balance as the Summer API. Studio shows usage by day and capability, and how much of each cap is used.
- **Player privacy.** Summer logs each call without the player's words. Players can report a reply, and Summer keeps it as evidence.
- **The game never breaks.** Every refusal has a stable error tag and a one-line fix, and the game shows its own offline line. See [errors](/router/errors).

## Who calls

In a single-player game, the player's game calls `Summer.client.router`. In a multiplayer game too: the player's game calls, and your server checks the result before it changes the shared world. See [server and client](/router/server-and-client).
