**Preview.** Realtime voice is the last part of Summer Router to ship. Until it does, `talk(..., {"mode": "voice"})` runs in text mode.
</Warning>

```gdscript
var s := await Summer.client.router.talk("guard", {"mode": "voice"})
s.tools = {"open_gate": _on_open_gate}
s.npc_text.connect(_on_npc_text)
talk_button.button_down.connect(func(): s.push_to_talk(true))
talk_button.button_up.connect(func(): s.push_to_talk(false))
```

Or put a `SummerRouterCharacter` node in the scene with `mode = "voice"`; it runs the whole flow. See [GDScript](/router/gdscript).

## How a voice session runs

1. The session starts in text mode. The character can already speak.
2. The player taps Talk. If the mic is not allowed yet, Summer shows its primer and the system prompt. See [Microphone permissions](/router/permissions).
3. With the mic allowed, the session switches to voice (`mode_changed`). The player's speech streams to Summer; the character's voice plays back.
4. Tool calls run your handlers on the player's device; the result goes back to the character.
5. The session ends on `end()`, `session.max_seconds`, `session.idle_seconds`, a cap, or the player leaving. `ended` carries the reason.

## End reasons

`game_closed`, `player_left`, `max_seconds`, `idle`, `limit_reached`, `credits`, `moderation`, `provider_error`, `server_shutdown`. Use the fallback on `limit_reached`, `credits`, `moderation` and `provider_error`.

## Rules

- Realtime voice needs a confirmed adult (`age_restricted` for others; they get text).
- Turn-taking comes from the character file: `turn.mode` `push_to_talk` (no echo from speakers) or `server_vad` (open mic). See [Characters](/router/characters).
- Before the session ends, Summer may send `limit_warning` with the seconds left.
