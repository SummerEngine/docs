Summer Engine is the game engine built for AI. Connect Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Grok or any
other AI agent with one command, describe the game you want, and your agent builds it: scenes,
code and assets, right inside the engine.

Every **Summer game** comes with multiplayer built in. Summer runs the servers, matchmaking,
parties, saves, leaderboards and store, so your agent can make a full online game, not a demo.
When it is ready, your agent publishes it on [Summer Games](https://summer.games) and you approve
it with one click.

Prefer to stay in the app? **Summer Agent**, the AI built into Summer Engine, does the same work
from a chat next to your game.

**Why Summer Engine:**
- **Built for agents**: one command connects your agent, and it drives the engine directly: it builds scenes, runs the game, reads errors and fixes them.
- **Multiplayer built in**: servers, matchmaking, parties, saves, leaderboards, chat and a store through the **Summer SDK**.
- **Publish in one step**: free to publish on Summer Games; your agent makes the store page and uploads the build.
- **A full engine for full games**: 2D and 3D, physics, animation and real code in C++, C# or scripts. Ship to desktop, mobile and the web.
- **Your project is yours**: plain project files you can read, edit and keep in Git.
- **Privacy controls**: Pro accounts can turn on Privacy Mode to keep their work out of training data.

## For Pros and New Game Devs

Summer Engine is for everyone who makes games: describe a game in plain words, or take full control of a professional engine.

### For Creators & Vibe Coders
- **Describe it, play it**: tell your agent the game you want and play it minutes later
- **Art and sound included**: generate 3D models, characters, textures, sprites and audio in the engine
- **Online from day one**: multiplayer, saves and leaderboards without running a server
- **Focus on fun**: skip the boilerplate and get straight to the gameplay

### For Professional Developers
- **Ship commercial games**: We shipped *Don't Pray* on Steam in 2.5 months using Summer Engine
- **Full engine access**: total control over every node and script, with native C++ and C# when you need it
- **Clean code**: your agent writes structured code you can read and own
- **Faster everything**: hand the tedious work to agents and spend your time on design and polish

Agents do the heavy lifting, and you can go as deep as you want.
