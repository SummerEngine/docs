## Build

- **The editor, with AI built in.** Summer's agent builds scenes, writes code, adds art and sound, then plays the game to check its work. [Work with Summer's agent](/quickstarts/ai-game-dev)
- **Or bring your own agent.** Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Hermes and more get the same tools through the Summer MCP and the `summer` CLI. [Agents overview](/mcp/overview)
- **175,000+ free assets.** 3D models, textures, sprites and sounds, ready to drop into your game. [Asset library](/art-system/gallery)
- **The Summer API.** Frontier generative models for images, 3D models, video, voice, sound effects and music, at API cost with zero markup. [Summer API](/api/overview)
- **Build together on the Summer Board.** A live board for your team and your agents: talk, draw, and your agents jump in and start working. Bring your own agent or use Summer's. [Summer Board](/mcp/operations/board)
- **Open and extendable.** Build your own plugins, and let your agent build custom tools in Summer Studio, such as a quest maker or an audio designer, that your whole team uses. [Summer Apps](/apps/overview) · [Plugins](/extending/overview)
- **Godot projects open in Summer.** Summer follows upstream Godot, so you can bring the project you already have. [Bring your project](/knowledge-base/godot-compatibility)

## Play together

- **Multiplayer primitives and servers.** The Summer SDK gives your game matchmaking, parties, saves and leaderboards, and Summer runs a server for every match. [Multiplayer](/build/multiplayer)
- **Cross-play.** On Summer Games, players on iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows play together. Cross-play on Steam and itch.io is coming.
- **AI inside your game.** The Summer Router adds AI characters, text to speech and images to games published on Summer Games. [Summer Router](/router/overview)
- **Every platform.** Windows, Mac, Linux, iOS, Android and the web. PlayStation and Xbox take some more custom work. [Platforms](/publishing/overview) · [Consoles](/publishing/consoles)

## Launch and grow

- **Publish on Summer Games, hands-free.** Your agent makes the store page and uploads the build. After your yes, the game is live as soon as Summer's automatic checks pass. [Publish with your agent](/publishing/agent-publishing)
- **Games from any engine.** Upload games made with Unity, Unreal, Three.js or Godot to Summer Games too. [Publish from Studio](/grow/publish)
- **Analytics for your game.** Track every event in your game, and let any agent read the numbers through the MCP, so you learn and make the game better. [Analytics](/build/analytics)
- **Summer Grow.** Tools to make organic content and ads for your game, built on what works across every game we make. [Summer Grow](/grow/overview)
- **Sparks and the creator program.** Players buy Sparks once and spend them in any Summer game. You earn from the Sparks spent in yours. [Sell items for Sparks](/build/store) · [Earnings](/grow/earnings)

## What it costs

- **The engine and the MCP are free.** Pay for what you use: a subscription gives you the frontier models in one place, or top up any amount. [Pricing](/essentials/pricing)
- **30% only when you sell.** Summer takes 30% of each sale. If you don't sell, you pay nothing. [What publishing costs](/publishing/cost)
