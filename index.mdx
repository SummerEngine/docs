Summer is the AI game engine built for people and agents to make games together. Not just to build
a game, but to launch it, learn from it and make it successful. Every tool for that is here, in one place.

**For people**, Summer Engine is an editor with Summer Agent built in. It has the same tools your own
agent gets. **For agents**, the Summer MCP and CLI reach everything: bring Claude Code, Codex, Cursor,
Hermes or any agent you like, add one URL and sign in once.

**Make it.** A full engine for full 2D and 3D games: physics, animation, real code in GDScript, C++ or C#.
Your agent makes images, 3D models, textures, sound effects, music and cutscene video with
the frontier generative models, at API cost, plus more than 175,000 free assets. Summer is built on
core Godot and follows upstream, so you can bring a Godot project and keep building it here. It is
open: build your own plugins, and let your agent build custom tools for your game, like a quest maker
or an audio designer, right inside Summer Studio for your whole team.

**Make it together.** On the **Summer Board** you, your friends and your agents work on one live board.
Talk, draw, and your agent jumps in and starts working. Use Summer Agent or bring your own.

**Make it multiplayer.** Every **Summer game** can go online with the **Summer SDK**: Summer runs the servers, matchmaking, parties, saves, leaderboards and store.
Start from a multiplayer template and make it your own. One build plays across iPhone, Android, Mac
and Windows on Summer Games, and cross-play on Steam and itch.io is coming.

**Launch it anywhere.** Windows, Mac, Linux, iOS, Android and the web; consoles take extra porting
work. Your agent publishes on [Summer Games](https://summer.games) hands-free through the MCP: it makes
the store page, uploads the build, and the game goes live once you say yes and Summer's checks pass.
Steam, itch.io or your own site work too, and Summer Games also takes builds made in other engines.

**Grow it.** Track everything that happens in your game, and let your agent read the analytics, talk
with your players and make the game better. Tools for organic content and ads are rolling out.

**Fair by design.** The engine and the MCP are free. You pay only for what you use: a plan with every
frontier model in one place, or top up any amount. When your game sells on Summer Games, Summer takes
30% of sales. If it does not sell, you pay nothing for that. We win when you win.

## For Pros and New Game Devs

Summer Engine is for everyone who makes games: describe a game in plain words, or take full control of a professional engine.

### For Creators & Vibe Coders
- **Describe it, play it**: tell your agent the game you want and play it minutes later
- **Art and sound included**: generate 3D models, characters, textures, sprites and audio in the engine
- **Online from day one**: multiplayer, saves and leaderboards without running a server
- **Focus on fun**: skip the boilerplate and get straight to the gameplay

### For Professional Developers
- **Ship commercial games**: We shipped *Don't Pray* on Steam in 2.5 months using Summer Engine
- **Full engine access**: total control over every node and script, with native C++ and C# when you need it
- **Clean code**: your agent writes structured code you can read and own
- **Faster everything**: hand the tedious work to agents and spend your time on design and polish

Agents do the heavy lifting, and you can go as deep as you want.
