Summer Engine is where you build the game. **Summer Studio** is everything around it. It is the place where all the frontier generative models are combined, where you and your team work with your agents on the Summer Board, where your agent builds custom tools for your game, and where you publish your game and grow it.

You find Studio at [summerengine.com/studio](https://www.summerengine.com/studio), and in the Summer app as the **Studio** tab, so you never leave the engine to use it. It is the same Studio, with the same account, the same team and the same data.

And it is built for agents as much as for people. Every Studio page, field and button can be read and used by your agent through the Summer MCP, so it can open a page, fill a form or start a generation for you. When something matters (paying, deleting, publishing, inviting, signing out), Studio opens the page and you press the button yourself.

## Make: every generative model in one place

The **Create** tools put the best models for game assets side by side, so you do not need an account at every AI company:

| Tool | What you make |
|---|---|
| **2D** | Concept art, sprites, icons, textures, UI and store art. |
| **3D** | Characters, props and environments as real 3D models, ready to import. |
| **Audio** | Sound effects, music and voice lines. |
| **Video** | Cutscenes, trailers and animated loops. |
| **Animation** | Animations for your characters. |

You pay only for what you use. What you make lands in **My Assets**, sorted into folders, and Summer Engine imports any of it into your game. Your agent reaches the same models through the [Summer API](/api/models), so it can make a whole set of assets for your game while you play.

Next to that, the **Asset Store** has more than 175,000 free assets you can use right away. See [Game assets in Summer Studio](/art-system/overview).

## Make it together: the Summer Board

The **Board** is one live board for each game. You, your friends and your agents work on it at the same time. Talk and draw on it, and an agent picks it up and starts working: your Summer agent, or your own Claude Code, Codex or any agent you like. See [Summer Board](/studio/board).

## Build your own tools: Summer Apps

Every game needs its own tools: a quest maker, an item database, a weapon balance table, a sound picker. In Studio, your agent builds them for you as **Summer Apps**: custom tools and custom UI that your whole team uses, live, in one place. Their data is an API your agents can read and write too. See [Build your own tools](/apps/custom-tools).

## Publish and grow: Summer Grow

**Grow** has every tool you need after the game is built: publish on Summer Games, see how it does, earn from it and grow it. Store pages and builds, analytics, earnings and payouts, prices and promotions, A/B tests, dashboards, alerts, community and content for social media. Your agent reads all of it through the MCP, so it can tell you how your game is doing and make it better. See [What is Summer Grow?](/grow/overview).

## For agents

Your agent sees Studio as a map of pages it can open, read and fill:

- `studio.map` lists every page and what it is for.
- `studio.open` moves the person's open Studio tab to a page.
- `studio.page` reads the open page: its fields and buttons.
- `studio.read_form` and `studio.submit_form` fill and save a form. Buttons that pay, delete, publish, invite or sign out stay the person's.

Every input: [Studio operations](/mcp/operations/studio). Connect your agent: [MCP setup](/mcp/setup).
