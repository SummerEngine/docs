## Start in Discord

Ask in the Summer Discord: [discord.gg/summerengine](https://discord.gg/summerengine). That is where you get the fastest reply and support. The Summer team is there, and so are many people who build with Summer every day.

## Where to get help

**1. Discord.** [discord.gg/summerengine](https://discord.gg/summerengine). Best for quick questions, bugs you can show in a screenshot, sharing work and getting feedback.

**2. Email for concrete help.** [support@summerengine.com](mailto:support@summerengine.com). Best for account and billing questions, detailed bug reports, or anything that needs a written thread.

**3. Email the founders.** [founders@summerengine.com](mailto:founders@summerengine.com). For partnerships, press, or anything you want the founders to see.

**Documentation.** You are reading it. The Knowledge Base, quickstarts, guides and [skills](/mcp/skills) cover most common questions. Agents can read these docs too, through the docs MCP and `llms.txt`.

## What to include

**For bugs:** what you were doing, what you expected and what happened. Add screenshots or error messages, your OS and your Summer Engine version.

**For feature requests:** describe the use case. What are you trying to do, and why would this help?

**For account and billing:** write from the email linked to your Summer account.

## Response times

Discord is usually fastest. Someone from the team or the community often answers within minutes. We aim to answer email within 24 hours on business days.

## Security issues

Do not post security issues in Discord. Email [support@summerengine.com](mailto:support@summerengine.com) or [founders@summerengine.com](mailto:founders@summerengine.com) with "SECURITY" in the subject. See [Security](/security/overview).
