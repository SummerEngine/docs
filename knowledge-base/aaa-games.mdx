**The engine does not cap your scope. Your team and your time do.** Summer Engine is a full game engine, and AI agents take on much of the work that used to need a big studio.

- **A full engine**: 2D and 3D, physics, animation, GDScript and native C++ and C#. Export to desktop, mobile and the web.
- **Agents do the heavy lifting**: they build scenes, write code, make assets and test the game. You spend your time on design and polish.
- **Online play is run for you**: Summer runs the servers, matchmaking, parties, saves and leaderboards, so you do not build that backend yourself.
- **Real results**: we built *Don't Pray*, an online PvP shooter on Steam, with Summer Engine in 2.5 months.

AI-generated art is strong for props, environments and fast iteration. For hero characters and key art at a big-budget quality bar, plan for artist time. See [How good are AI-generated assets?](/knowledge-base/ai-asset-quality).
