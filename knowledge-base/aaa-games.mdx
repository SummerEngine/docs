Summer is a full game engine: 2D and 3D, physics, animation, native C++ and C#, and export to desktop, mobile and the web. The same engine foundation powers commercial games on every platform.

What changes is who does the work. AI agents build scenes, code and assets, and Summer runs the servers for online play. A small team can now build a game that used to need a big studio. How big your game gets depends on your team and your time, not on the engine.

We shipped *Don't Pray* on Steam in 2.5 months with Summer Engine.
