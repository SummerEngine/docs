**Your project stays yours, and we never sell it.** Your game is plain project files on your computer. Summer reads and sends only what it needs to answer you.

## What is always true

- **Encryption.** Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
- **No selling your data.** We do not sell your code, prompts or game projects to third parties.
- **AI providers see what you send.** When you chat with Summer AI, your prompts and the code snippets in them go to our model providers to generate the answer.
- **Project search runs on our servers.** To find the right files, Summer stores chunks of your changed files with their paths. See [How does project intelligence work?](/knowledge-base/project-intelligence).
- **Your own agent, your provider's terms.** When Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another agent drives Summer Engine through local MCP, that agent's provider handles your prompts under its own terms.

## Training use

The [Privacy Policy](https://summerengine.com/privacy), section "How we use your prompts and code", is the binding text. In short:

- On the Free plan, prompts and outputs may be used to improve our models.
- The policy describes Privacy Mode for paid plans. See [Privacy Mode](/security/privacy-mode).
- Content flagged for safety or security review, and content you send us as feedback or in a bug report, may be kept and reviewed in every case.
