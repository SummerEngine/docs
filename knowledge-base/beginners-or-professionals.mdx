**Both.** Summer Engine is the game engine built for AI. The same engine works if you describe a game in plain words or if you write every line yourself.

- **For beginners**: describe the game you want. Your agent writes the code, builds the scenes and makes the assets. You play it and ask for changes. No coding needed.
- **For professionals**: a full 2D and 3D engine with physics, animation, GDScript, and native C++ and C#. Your project is plain files you keep in Git. Hand the repetitive work to agents and keep full control of the architecture.
- **For both**: Summer runs the servers, matchmaking and saves for online games, and you publish on Summer Games, Steam or your own site.

We built *Don't Pray*, our own game on Steam, with Summer Engine in 2.5 months. See [Has Summer been used for production games?](/knowledge-base/production-games).
