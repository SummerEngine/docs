**Yes, you can make and export games for free.** The Summer Engine desktop app is free to download and use. You pay only for hosted Summer services: Summer AI, asset generation and other cloud work.

- **Free plan**: no credit card. A few hosted AI messages each day and limited AI asset creations.
- **Bring your own agent for free**: local MCP workflows stay free. Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another agent drives Summer Engine on your own subscription.
- **Paid plans**: Pro ($20/mo), Pro+ ($60/mo) and Ultra ($200/mo) raise hosted AI and generation limits. Yearly billing saves 20%. A Basic plan ($5/mo) is offered inside the app.
- **Past your limit**: add credits or turn on auto top-up. You do not have to change plan.

See [Pricing](/essentials/pricing) for the full plan table, and [Account & Billing](/essentials/account-billing) for credits, auto top-up and spend limits. For what is open source, free and paid, see [What is open in Summer Engine?](/knowledge-base/source-status).
