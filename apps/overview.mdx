A **Summer App** is a tool for your game team that lives inside Summer Studio. Your agent builds it in minutes; your whole team uses it.

Examples: a loot and balance table that writes your game's data file, a quest editor, a board that tracks every creature from concept to in-engine, a grid to pick the best of three sound effects, a live-ops view of your game.

## What an app is

| | |
|---|---|
| **Shared** | An app belongs to a team (workspace). Everyone in the team can open it and sees the same data, live. |
| **Real code** | An app is a small web page (HTML, JS, CSS) built with the Summer SDK and UI kit, or a quick JSON layout. |
| **Live on your work** | Apps read and change your Summer assets, generate new ones, read your game analytics, and talk to Summer Engine. |
| **For agents too** | An app's data is an API your agent reads and writes, and an app can give your agent new tools. See [Agents and apps](/apps/agents). |
| **Versioned** | Every change is a new version. Going back is one click. |
| **Safe** | Apps run sandboxed, with only the permissions they declare. See [Security](/apps/security). |

## Where apps live

- **Studio > Apps** lists your team's apps: pinned, scratch and your folders.
- An app opens at `summerengine.com/studio/apps/<appId>`.
- New apps start in **Scratch**. Click **Keep** to move one into your team's apps. Scratch apps nobody opens for 14 days are archived and can be restored for 30 more days.
- In Summer Engine, apps open in the Studio tab and can dock next to your scene. See [Engine and docks](/apps/engine-and-docks).

## How to get one

Ask your agent. In Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or the Summer Studio chat:

<Prompt description="**Build a team tool.** Your agent builds a Summer App and opens it in your Studio tab.">
Build us a Summer App: a loot balance table for res://data/items.json that shows
damage, speed and DPS per item, flags items over 60 DPS, and writes changes back
to the file. Read summer_app_guide first.
</Prompt>
