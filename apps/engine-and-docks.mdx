## How an app reaches your engine

- **Inside Summer Engine** (the Studio tab or a dock), engine calls go straight to your open project.
- **In a browser**, they reach your running Summer Engine through Summer when your desktop app is online and signed in. Otherwise the call fails with `engine_offline` and the app shows "Open in Summer Engine".

An app needs `engine:read` or `engine:write` in its [manifest](/apps/manifest), and it may read and write only the files it declares in `engineFiles` (plus `res://addons/<plugin>/` when it declares a `plugin`).

## What an app may do

Allowed operations: add, set, remove, move, reparent and replace nodes; instantiate scenes; set resource properties; connect signals; open and save scenes; undo; import from a URL; grep and search files; viewport, game, world and scene snapshots; play and stop; select nodes; navigate the editor.

Never allowed from an app: running scripts or shell commands, deleting or moving files, writing files outside the declared paths, editor-window screenshots, or writing script source into a scene. These rules are enforced by Summer and again inside the engine.

## Docks

Any app can be docked as an editor panel (left, right or bottom). Docks stay in your editor layout per project. Ask your agent, or use the npm tool:

```json
{ "action": "open", "appId": "<app id>", "slot": "bottom" }
```

`summer_app_dock` also closes a dock (`"action": "close"`) and lists docks (`"action": "list"`). On engine builds without app docks it answers `engine_lacks_op`; open the app in the Studio tab instead.

## Plugins

An agent can build an editor plugin (`res://addons/<name>/`) together with an app; the plugin and the app message each other through the engine (`summer.engine.plugin.send` and `onMessage` in the app).
