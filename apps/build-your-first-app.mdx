You need:

- A Summer account, signed in to Summer Studio in your browser or in Summer Engine.
- An agent with the Summer Engine MCP. One command sets it up: see [Connect your agent](/publishing/connect-your-agent) or [Claude Code](/mcp/claude-code).

## 1. Ask for the tool

Open Summer Studio, then ask your agent:

<Prompt description="**Loot table.** A shared table of your items with DPS, written back to your game.">
Build us a Summer App called "Loot table": every item from res://data/items.json
with damage, speed and DPS, a DPS chart that flags items over 60, and a
"Write to game" button that saves the file. Read summer_app_guide and follow
the summer-apps and summer-apps-design skills.
</Prompt>

## 2. What your agent does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reads the guide">
    `summer_app_guide` returns the build loop, the SDK, the manifest, the UI kit, the design rules and worked examples.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Creates the app">
    `summer_app_create` makes a scratch app in your workspace. Your open Studio tab moves to it with the notice "Your agent built Loot table".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Writes the files">
    An `index.html`, its JavaScript and CSS, and a manifest that declares what the app may do (here: shared data and reading and writing `res://data/items.json`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publishes">
    `summer_app_publish` uploads the files as version 1. Your tab shows the files arriving, then boots the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Listens">
    `summer_app_events` waits for what you do in the app (for example "Write to game") and for errors, so the agent can react.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. Use it with your team

- Everyone in the workspace can open it from **Studio > Apps**. Edits show up for everyone live.
- Click **Keep** so it does not expire with the scratch apps.
- Pin it to your sidebar.

## 4. Change it

Ask again. Your agent reads the current files with `summer_app_get`, changes them and publishes version 2. The open app reloads on the new version. **Versions** on the app page goes back to any earlier one.

<Prompt description="**Change an app.** Your agent edits the app and publishes a new version.">
In our Loot table app, add a column for price and sort by DPS by default.
</Prompt>

## 5. Let your agent use the app's data

You do not need the app open for your agent to read or change its data:

<Prompt description="**Data as API.** Your agent edits the app's shared data; everyone sees it live.">
In our Loot table app, lower the bow's damage by 10% and tell me its new DPS.
</Prompt>

More in [Agents and apps](/apps/agents).

## If something goes wrong

| You see | Do |
|---|---|
| The agent says `not_found` | Summer Apps is not open for your account yet, or the app is in a workspace you are not in. |
| A publish error names a file | Ask the agent to fix that file and publish again. |
| The app shows an error | The agent reads it with `summer_app_events` (kind `error`). Ask it to fix the app. |
| "Open in Summer Engine" in the app | The app needs your engine. Open the app in Summer Engine's Studio tab. |
