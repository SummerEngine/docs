## First: does Summer already do it?

An app is something your team has to keep. Your agent first checks whether Summer already does the job: Studio features (the asset library with collections and groups, Pick one and sound choices, Grow dashboards, Team) and the Summer MCP tools. If Summer does it, the agent uses that and tells you where it is. It builds an app only when the need is real and missing, or as a Scratch throwaway that it calls a throwaway and archives with `summer_app_update` when you are done with it. When something is missing, it reports it to Summer with `summer_app_report_gap`.

| You ask | Your agent |
|---|---|
| "Let me pick from these 12 sword sounds" | Uses Pick one, or puts the sounds in a library collection. No app. |
| "Group our assets into a moodboard for the forest level" | Uses the asset library's collections and groups. No app. |
| "A weapon balance table with our DPS budget" | Builds an app: nothing in Summer edits game data with your balance rules. |

## Before you start

You need:

- A Summer account, signed in to Summer Studio in your browser or in Summer Engine.
- An agent with the Summer Engine MCP: the hosted MCP at `https://mcp.summerengine.com/mcp` (nothing to install), or the `summer-engine` npm MCP if the agent also edits your project. See [Connect your agent](/apps/build-with-your-agent#connect-your-agent).
- Or no agent of your own: describe the tool to Summer in Studio chat. **Build a new app** in **Studio > Apps** opens it.

## 1. Ask for the tool

Open Summer Studio, then ask your agent (or paste the same text into Studio chat):

<Prompt description="**Loot table.** A shared table of your items with DPS, kept in Summer.">
Build us a Summer App called "Loot table": our items with damage, speed and DPS,
kept in the app's shared data, and a DPS chart that flags items over 60. Seed it with
our items using summer_app_data. Read summer_app_guide first, follow the summer-apps
and summer-apps-design skills, and check the app with summer_app_screenshot (also at
width 390 and in dark theme) before you tell me it is done.
</Prompt>

## 2. What your agent does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reads the guide">
    `summer_app_guide` returns the build loop, the SDK, the manifest, the UI kit, the design rules and worked examples.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Creates the app">
    `summer_app_create` makes a Scratch app in your workspace. Your open Studio tab moves to it with the notice "Your agent built Loot table".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Writes the files">
    An `index.html`, its JavaScript, and a manifest that declares what the app may do. Here: shared data (`data`), with an `items` collection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publishes">
    `summer_app_publish` uploads the files as version 1. Your tab shows the files arriving, then starts the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Seeds the data">
    `summer_app_data` writes your items into the app's `items` collection, as the team's starter data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Looks at it">
    `summer_app_screenshot` renders the app with your data and reports errors. The agent fixes them and publishes again until the app is clean in light, dark and at phone width.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Listens">
    `summer_app_events` waits for what you do in the app and for errors, so the agent can react.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. Use it with your team

- Everyone in the workspace can open it from **Studio > Apps**. Edits show up for everyone, live.
- Choose **Keep** in the app's menu, so it does not expire with the Scratch apps. Your agent can do it too: `summer_app_update` with `status: "kept"`.
- Choose **Pin to sidebar** to keep it one click away.

## 4. Change it

Ask again, or choose **Edit with agent** on the app page to change it in Studio chat. Your agent reads the current files with `summer_app_get`, changes them and publishes version 2. The open app reloads on the new version. **Versions** on the app page lists every version; **Make live** goes back to one.

<Prompt description="**Change an app.** Your agent edits the app and publishes a new version.">
In our Loot table app, add a column for price and sort by DPS by default.
</Prompt>

## 5. Let your agent use the app's data

Your agent does not need the app open to read or change its data:

<Prompt description="**Data as API.** Your agent edits the app's shared data; everyone sees it live.">
In our Loot table app, lower the bow's damage by 10% and tell me its new DPS.
</Prompt>

More in [Agents and apps](/apps/agents).

## If something goes wrong

| You see | Do |
|---|---|
| The agent has no `summer_app_*` tools, or gets `not_found` | Summer Apps is not open for your account yet, or the app is in a workspace you are not in. |
| A publish error names a file or a key | Ask the agent to fix it and publish again. |
| `permission_missing` | The manifest lacks a permission. Ask the agent to add it and publish again. |
| The app shows an error | The agent reads it with `summer_app_events` (kind `error`) or `summer_app_screenshot`. Ask it to fix the app. |
