**Publishing is free.** Then you choose how you earn. These are the same three options as on [summer.games/publish](https://summer.games/publish).

<img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/guide-art/publishing/cost-light.svg" alt="Three ways to earn: Free, players can tip you, any engine. 30%, set a price and Summer takes 30% of each sale, any engine. Sparks, sell in-game items for Sparks, games built in Summer only." />
<img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/guide-art/publishing/cost-dark.svg" alt="Three ways to earn: Free, players can tip you, any engine. 30%, set a price and Summer takes 30% of each sale, any engine. Sparks, sell in-game items for Sparks, games built in Summer only." />

| Option | What it costs you | For |
|---|---|---|
| **Free, with tips** | Nothing. Players who like your game can send you a tip. | Any engine |
| **Set a price** | Summer takes **30%** of each sale. You choose the price. | Any engine |
| **The Summer economy** | Opt in to Sparks and sell in-game items from your own item shop, next to your game page. | Games built in Summer only |

Sparks are Summer's in-game currency. Players buy Sparks and spend them on items in games across Summer.

## How to set it up

- **Tips and a price** are set on your store page in Studio, not in your game's code. See [Charge for your game and accept tips](/build/paid-games).
- **Items for Sparks** are set up in your game's catalog and in your game. See [Sell items for Sparks](/build/store).
- **A price needs payouts.** In Studio, open **Get paid** and connect your payout account. Until then your game stays free.

## When your agent publishes

Your agent can propose a price when it submits. The price is set only when you click **Approve publishing**, and only if your payouts are set up. See [Approve publishing](/publishing/owner-approval).

## Other costs

- **Store art made with Summer** uses your Summer credits, like any other generation. Your own art and screenshots cost nothing.
- **Analytics:** every game keeps 10,000 custom events for 7 days for free. See [See what players do](/build/analytics).

The full terms are in the [Creator Program Terms](https://summerengine.com/legal/creator-program).
