**Yes.** Your AI agent can do every step, from the finished game to a published store page. Six switches in **Studio > Settings > [Agent permissions](/mcp/permissions)** say what it may do without asking.

Your agent always asks you before the game goes live. After your yes, the game is live as soon as Summer's automatic checks pass. There is no review to wait for.

## What your agent does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Finds your game">
    It lists your Summer projects and your store games, or makes a new store game with your title and platforms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Exports the build">
    It exports your game with Summer Engine on your computer, first, so a problem shows up before any art is made.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fills the art">
    It uses the art you already have in My assets, or makes new art with Summer, crops it to each slot and uploads it. Screenshots are real gameplay. See [Make store art with your agent](/publishing/generate-store-art).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Writes the store page">
    A tagline, descriptions for the desktop and mobile stores, tags and the age rating, from what your game really does.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Uploads the build">
    Summer checks it on every platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Asks you, then goes live">
    It shows you what will go live and asks. After your yes, it takes the game live with `summer_go_live`. The game is live as soon as Summer's automatic checks pass, and your agent gives you the page link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What only you do

| Step | Why |
|---|---|
| **Sign in** to your Summer account the first time | Your agent acts as you, so you say yes once in your browser. |
| **Say yes** before it goes live | Your agent always asks before a game goes live, and when a switch in Agent permissions is off. You see exactly what will change. Your agent cannot approve for you. |
| **Set up payouts** for a paid game | Your payout account is yours. |

There is no second click and no review: after your yes, the page and the build go live as soon as Summer's automatic checks pass.

## Ask your agent

Connect your agent first: [Connect your agent](/publishing/connect-your-agent). Then paste the master prompt from [How do I publish my game on Summer?](/publishing/summer-games#let-your-agent-publish-it), or this shorter one:

<Prompt description="**Publish my game on Summer Games.** Your agent does every step and asks you before it goes live.">
Publish my Summer game on Summer Games. Use the Summer Engine MCP and the
`publish-your-game` skill, and follow
https://docs.summerengine.com/publishing/agent-checklist.md from top to bottom.
Ask me only what my project does not say. Ask me before the game goes live,
and stop when it is live.
</Prompt>

## Is it safe?

- **Six switches set the limits.** **Make and build**, **Spend credits**, **Publish** and **Prices and live changes** are on by default. **Delete for good** and **Contact people** are off, so your agent asks first. These defaults apply now. You can change them soon. Until then, price, A/B test and member changes ask you first. Going live always asks you.
- **When a switch is off, your agent asks.** You get a link (`https://www.summerengine.com/studio/approvals/<id>`) and a card in your open Studio tab. Nothing changes until you approve.
- **You see what agents did.** **Studio > Settings > Agent activity** lists every change, newest first, with Undo for prices, sales, country prices, member roles, team names and the event limit.
- **Summer checks every build** before players get it: the game goes live only when the automatic checks pass.
- **The price is yours.** Your agent sets the price you asked for. A paid game needs your payouts set up.
- **Art uses your credits.** Your agent spends within your credits and auto top-up limit, so it checks My assets first.

Details: [Agent permissions](/mcp/permissions) and [Approvals](/publishing/owner-approval).
