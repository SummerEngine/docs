You publish a game on Summer Games in five steps:

1. **Export** your game from Summer Engine as one file.
2. **Make its store page**: text and art for each store your game is on.
3. **Upload** the build. Summer checks it on every platform you picked.
4. **Submit** the page and the build.
5. **Summer reviews it.** When it is approved, you go live on summer.games.

You can do this in two ways. The result is the same.

## What players get

Your game gets its own page at `summer.games/games/<your-game>`. Players find it on the summer.games home page, in the Summer Games desktop app for Mac and Windows, and in the Summer Games apps for iPhone and Android, depending on the platforms you pick.

Nothing reaches players until Summer has approved your page and you go live. Saving a draft, uploading a build or submitting never makes the game public by itself.
