You publish a game on Summer Games in five steps: **export** it, add its **art**, write its **store page**, **upload** the build, and **publish**. Export comes first: a build that does not export stops everything, so you find out before you make art. Then Summer reviews it, and it goes live on summer.games.

You can do it yourself in Summer Studio, or let your AI agent do every step: Summer Engine is the game engine built for AI. The result is the same.

## Where you upload

There is one place to upload a game: **[summerengine.com/studio/upload](https://www.summerengine.com/studio/upload)**. After that, your game is in **Your games** at [summerengine.com/studio/store](https://www.summerengine.com/studio/store). There you change its store page, art and versions, and submit it.

summer.games is where players play. Its **Upload** button brings you to the same Studio page. Your agent works on the same store game through the Summer Engine MCP.

## Let your agent publish it

[Connect your agent](/publishing/connect-your-agent) to the Summer Engine MCP, open your game's folder, and paste this:

<Prompt description="**Publish my game to Summer Games.** Your agent does every step and asks you before it goes live.">
Publish my game to Summer Games. Use the Summer Engine MCP and its
`publish-your-game` skill, and follow
https://docs.summerengine.com/publishing/agent-checklist.md from top to bottom.

1. Read my project and its plan. Ask me only what they don't say: which
   platforms, and free or which price.
2. Find or create the store game, then export the game for my platforms.
3. Fill every art slot. Use my art in My assets first. Make only what is
   missing, in each slot's own shape, crop it yourself, and upload it. No
   text in key art, covers or icons. Screenshots are real gameplay.
4. Fill the store page for every store my platforms need: tagline,
   descriptions, tags and age rating. Describe only what the game really has.
5. Upload the build. Show me what you will upload before you upload it.
6. Check the store page's checklist until nothing is missing. Send me a
   private preview link.
7. Ask me before it goes live. After my yes, take it live, and tell me it is
   live only when Summer says so.
</Prompt>

Your agent asks you before the game goes live. After your yes, it is live as soon as Summer's automatic checks pass. Six switches in [Agent permissions](/mcp/permissions) say what else it may do without asking. See [Can my AI agent do it for me?](/publishing/agent-publishing)

## Do it yourself in Studio

Export in the editor, then open [summerengine.com/studio/upload](https://www.summerengine.com/studio/upload) and fill your store page with a live preview next to the form. See [Publish your game](/build/publish), and [Publish your game from Summer Studio](/grow/publish) for every Studio step.

## What players get

Your game gets its own page at `summer.games/games/<your-game>`. Players find it on summer.games, in the Summer Games desktop app for Mac and Windows, and in the Summer Games apps for iPhone and Android, depending on the platforms you pick.

Nothing reaches players until you publish it and Summer's automatic checks pass. There is no review to wait for. Saving a draft or uploading a build never makes the game public by itself.
