Your agent can fill every art slot on your store page. It uses your own art first and makes new art only for the slots that are empty.

## The order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read what is missing">
    `summer_store_get_listing` lists every slot and what is in it. Add `previews: true` to see the current art.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check My assets">
    `summer_list_my_assets` and `summer_search_assets` find art you already have: key art, your hero, logos, screenshots. `summer_get_asset` shows one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the board">
    If your game has a board, `summer_board_read` and `summer_board_game_soul` hold its look, characters and world. New art should match them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make what is missing">
    `summer_generate_image` makes the picture, then `summer_check_job` waits for it. The new picture is saved in My assets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Crop it to the slot">
    The agent crops the picture to the slot's exact shape and size itself, keeping the subject at the focal point.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put it in the slot">
    `summer_store_set_art` with the slot id, the asset id or a URL, and the focal point. The tool checks the shape before it uploads.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Make one picture per shape

Ask the generator for the slot's own shape. Do not make one landscape picture and stretch it into every slot.

| Slot | Ask for | Prompt must say |
|---|---|---|
| `desktop.keyArt`, `mobile.keyArt` | 16:9, at least 1920×1080 | No text, no logo. Subject near the middle. |
| `mobile.tallCover` | 9:16, at least 1080×1920 | No text. Subject in the middle 4:5. Calm, dark bottom third. |
| `desktop.capsuleTall` | 3:4, at least 1200×1600 | Logo allowed, inside the middle 80%. |
| `desktop.capsuleWide` | 460:215, at least 920×430 | Logo allowed. |
| `desktop.icon`, `mobile.icon` | 1:1, 1024×1024 | No words. One clear silhouette on a plain background. |

**Screenshots are never generated.** They are real gameplay. Your agent can take them with the engine (`summer_screenshot`) or ask you to capture them.

## Crop it yourself

Generators return their own sizes. Your agent crops each picture to the slot itself:

1. Compute the slot's shape. For example, from a 2048×1152 picture to a 9:16 tall cover: height 1152, width 1152 × 9 ÷ 16 = 648.
2. Center the crop on the subject, then move it so it stays inside the picture.
3. Scale it up to the slot's minimum size if needed (1080×1920 for the tall cover). Better: generate at the right shape and size.
4. Save it as PNG or JPEG and put it in the slot (next section).

`summer_store_set_art` takes one of your asset ids or a public `https` image URL. The store fetches the picture itself, so no image passes through the chat.

```python title="crop_to_slot.py (Pillow)"
from PIL import Image

def crop_to_slot(path, out, ratio_w, ratio_h, min_w, min_h, focus_x=0.5, focus_y=0.5):
    img = Image.open(path).convert("RGB")
    w, h = img.size
    target = ratio_w / ratio_h
    if w / h > target:            # too wide: cut the sides
        cw, ch = round(h * target), h
    else:                         # too tall: cut top and bottom
        cw, ch = w, round(w / target)
    left = min(max(round(focus_x * w - cw / 2), 0), w - cw)
    top = min(max(round(focus_y * h - ch / 2), 0), h - ch)
    img = img.crop((left, top, left + cw, top + ch))
    if cw < min_w or ch < min_h:
        img = img.resize((max(min_w, cw), max(min_h, ch)), Image.LANCZOS)
    img.save(out, quality=92)

crop_to_slot("key-art.png", "tall-cover.jpg", 9, 16, 1080, 1920, focus_x=0.45)
```

Each slot's shape must match within 2%.

## Put it in the slot

```json title="One picture, with a focal point"
{
  "tool": "summer_store_set_art",
  "gameId": "<gameId>",
  "slot": "mobile.tallCover",
  "sources": [{ "assetId": "<assetId>" }],
  "focalPoint": { "x": 0.45, "y": 0.4 }
}
```

```json title="Add screenshots"
{
  "tool": "summer_store_set_art",
  "gameId": "<gameId>",
  "slot": "desktop.screenshots",
  "sources": [{ "assetId": "<shot1>" }, { "assetId": "<shot2>" }],
  "mode": "append"
}
```

- `focalPoint` is from 0 to 1, from the left and from the top. Key art, capsules and the tall cover only.
- `mode`: `replace` (default) or `append`. Screenshots take a list of up to 8; other slots take one picture.
- An empty `sources` list clears the slot.

## Rules

- Make art from **your** game: your hero, your world, your look. Never another game's characters. Never fake gameplay.
- Check My assets before you make anything. Making art uses your Summer credits.
- No text in key art, the tall cover, icons or screenshots.
- Stay inside the 20-picture budget across both stores.

Sizes and reasons for every slot: [Store art](/publishing/store-art).
