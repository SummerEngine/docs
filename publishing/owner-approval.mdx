Your agent asks you first in three cases:

- a switch in **Studio > Settings > [Agent permissions](/mcp/permissions)** is off;
- the change always asks: agent permissions, auto top-up, entering a jam, the analytics event limit;
- Summer Games still asks: for now, the game's owner approves an agent's publish or release, and you approve price, A/B test and member changes.

Your agent gives you a link. Nothing changes until you approve.

## Approve a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the link">
    Your agent gives you a link like `https://www.summerengine.com/studio/approvals/<id>`. If you have Studio open in a tab, the same request shows there as a card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check what will change">
    The request says what your agent wants to do, with each value before and after.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve, or turn it down">
    Approve it, and the change happens. Turn it down, and nothing changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A request for your own yes expires after 24 hours. A request for a game owner's yes expires after 7 days. **Studio > Settings > Agent activity** lists every request and every change, with Undo for prices, sales, country prices, member roles, team names and the event limit.

## Approve publishing

For now every agent publish asks the game's owner: the answer is `needs_approval` with the owner's approval link. Summer Games records exactly that version and waits for the owner.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the link">
    The link looks like `https://www.summerengine.com/studio/store/<gameId>/approve/<approvalId>`. Open it signed in to your Summer account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check what will publish">
    The page shows **What will publish**: the stores, the title, the desktop and mobile text, the build, the price and your agent's note. Below it is the **store page preview**: how players will see this version.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve, or not now">
    Click **Approve publishing** to send it to Summer for review. Click **Not now** to turn it down; nothing is published, and your agent can publish again later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you approve:

1. The price is set as you. A price needs payouts set up; without them nothing is submitted, and the page tells you.
2. The page goes to Summer for review.
3. When the review passes, the store page goes live. There is no second click. For a store bundle, your agent then releases the build you approved with `store.release`.

## When you publish yourself

If you click **Submit for review** in Studio yourself, that is your approval. There is no extra step. See [Publish your game](/build/publish).

## What the states mean

| State | Your agent sees | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Awaiting owner approval** | `awaiting_approval` | Your agent asked. Nothing is published. | Open the link. |
| **Approved** | `done` | You approved it. It went to Summer for review. | Wait for the review. |
| **Cancelled** | `cancelled` | You clicked **Not now**, or your agent withdrew it. | Ask your agent to publish again when ready. |
| **Stale** | `stale` | The store page changed after your agent asked. | Ask your agent to publish again, so you approve exactly what will publish. |

## What your agent may do

With the default switches, your agent can read your games, edit the draft, upload art and builds, and start builds without asking. These defaults apply now. You can change them soon. Until then, Summer Games still asks the game's owner before an agent publishes, and price, A/B test and member changes ask you first. It asks first before it deletes something for good or contacts people, such as a team invite.

Only you can set up payouts. On a game that is already live, only the owner can finish a web build or download.

Summer checks every agent call. A change Summer Games does not take from an agent yet comes back as `needs_approval` with a link for you.

## For agents

- After `summer_store_publish`, read `status`. `needs_approval` (for now every agent publish): give the owner `approval.url`, then call `summer_jobs_wait` with `job.id` until it is `done`, `cancelled` or `stale`. `done`: Summer's review runs next. Do not say the game is live.
- `summer_store_status` shows the request, the review and its feedback.
- If the job ends `stale`, read the store page again with `summer_store_get_listing` and publish again with a new `idempotencyKey`.
- After the review passes, the page goes live. For a store bundle, `store.release` (with an `idempotencyKey`) releases exactly the build the owner approved. Web builds and downloads need no release.
