Tick each item before you submit. Your agent can do every item except the ones marked **you**.

## The game

- [ ] **A Summer account**, signed in. **You** sign in once; your agent cannot sign in for you.
- [ ] **The platforms** your game ships on: web, Mac, Windows, Linux, iPhone, Android. They decide which stores your page needs. See [Export your game](/publishing/export).
- [ ] **A build that exports** without errors for those platforms, with a version such as `v1.0.0`.
- [ ] **The game starts** from its main scene and plays without errors.
- [ ] **A multiplayer game** has its server preset. See [Publish your game](/build/publish).

## The store page

The **desktop store** is summer.games and the Summer Games desktop app. The **mobile store** is the Summer Games apps for iPhone and Android. You need each store your platforms open.

| Item | Desktop store | Mobile store |
|---|---|---|
| Title | up to 60 characters, shared | shared |
| Tagline | up to 80 characters | its own, or "Same as desktop" |
| Description | 120-300 characters | 120-170 characters, or "Same as desktop" |
| Tags | 3-8 store categories | shared |
| Key art | 16:9, 1920×1080, no text | 16:9, 1920×1080, no text |
| Icon | square, 1024×1024, no words | square, 1024×1024, no words |
| Screenshots | at least 4, 16:9, real gameplay | at least 3, in the way the game is played |
| Tall cover | — | 9:16, 1080×1920, no text (strongly recommended) |
| Tall and wide capsules | optional | — |
| Trailer | optional | not shown yet |
| Age rating | Everyone, Everyone 10+ or Teen | shared |

Details and the reasons behind each rule: [Store art](/publishing/store-art) and [Store page text](/publishing/store-text).

## Money

- [ ] **Free, or a price.** Free is the default. Players can tip you. See [What does it cost?](/publishing/cost)
- [ ] **For a price: payouts set up.** **You** connect your payout account in Studio under **Get paid**. Until then your game stays free.

## Optional

- [ ] **Analytics events** for the moments you want to count, such as `level_completed`. Summer counts page views, launches and play time for you. See [Analytics in Grow](/publishing/analytics).
- [ ] **A private preview link** to show the page to a friend before you submit.

## The last step

- [ ] **You** click **Approve publishing** on the link your agent gives you, or **Submit for review** in Studio if you do it yourself. See [Approve publishing](/publishing/owner-approval).
