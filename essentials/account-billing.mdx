## Billing

### How do I access billing settings?

Open **Billing** in [Summer Studio](https://www.summerengine.com/studio?tab=billing). It shows your plan, included usage and limits. From Billing you open Stripe's secure portal for payment method, subscription, and invoice management.

### What are Summer Engine's billing cycles?

Billing cycles run monthly, starting on your subscription date. Included AI usage resets at renewal.

### Where can I find my invoices?

**Billing** in Summer Studio lists your invoices. The Stripe portal, opened from Billing, has your full billing history to view and download.

### How do I update my billing information?

Update payment method, address, and tax information through the Stripe portal (opened from **Billing** in Summer Studio). We use Stripe for secure transactions. Changes only affect future invoices.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel from **Billing** in Summer Studio. Access continues until the end of your current billing period.

### I'm having other billing issues. How can I get help?

Ask in [Discord](https://discord.gg/summerengine) first for the fastest reply. For anything about your account or payments, email [support@summerengine.com](mailto:support@summerengine.com) from the email linked to your account.

## Credits and auto top-up

Add credits at [summerengine.com/studio/credits](https://www.summerengine.com/studio/credits). It opens **Billing** in Summer Studio with **Add credits**.

**Auto top-up** keeps your work going. Turn it on in **Billing**, or in the **Add credits** step. You pick three amounts:

- the balance that starts a top-up: when your credits fall below it, Summer adds more;
- the amount Summer adds each time;
- a monthly limit: auto top-up never adds more than this in one month.

### Credits and your agents

Agents you connect, like Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code, Codex or Cursor, spend within your credits and your auto top-up monthly limit. They do not ask you for each call. An agent can never change auto top-up on its own: that always asks you. Soon you can make your agent ask before it spends: turn off **Spend credits** in [Agent permissions](/mcp/permissions).

When credits run out, the agent gets the error `no_credits` with one link: `https://www.summerengine.com/studio/credits`. In ChatGPT, it tells you in words.

## Spend Limits

Set spending limits to control costs and prevent unexpected charges. Spend limits help you manage usage and stay within budget.

Spend limits apply to **on-demand usage only**. Included usage in your plan does not count towards spend limits.

### Viewing spend limits

View your current spend limits in **Billing** in [Summer Studio](https://www.summerengine.com/studio?tab=billing).

On-demand usage must be enabled to view and set spend limits. Free users must upgrade to a paid plan first before they can enable on-demand.

### Updating spend limits

You can update spending limits at any time:

- **Increase limits**: Takes effect immediately
- **Decrease limits**: Takes effect immediately, but won't affect usage that has already occurred
- **Range**: the monthly on-demand limit is between $1 and $500

### Spend limit behavior

When your spending limit is reached:

- On-demand usage stops until the next billing cycle
- You see a notification that your limit was reached
- You can still add credits, raise the limit or upgrade your plan to keep working
- On-demand usage resumes at the start of the next billing cycle

### Individual plans

Customers with an active paid subscription (Basic, Pro, Pro+ or Ultra) and a saved payment method can turn on on-demand usage and set its monthly limit. Free users must upgrade first.
