## Creating Your Account

Summer Engine needs an account for hosted AI, asset generation, publishing and your connected agents.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Visit [summerengine.com](https://summerengine.com) and create a free account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify email">
    Check your email and click the verification link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in through Summer Engine">
    In Summer Engine, open any project and navigate to the 'Chat' menu in the sidebar. This will open a deeplink to authenticate your account automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Authentication Process

Summer Engine uses a streamlined authentication system:

1. **Open a Project**: Create or open any project in Summer Engine
2. **Access Chat**: Click on the 'Chat' menu in the sidebar
3. **Automatic Authentication**: Summer Engine will open a secure deeplink to authenticate your account
4. **Return to Summer Engine**: Once authenticated, you'll be redirected back to Summer Engine with full access

## Sign in your agent

There is no API key. Your agent signs in with your Summer account in the browser, once.

- **Hosted MCP** (Claude, ChatGPT and any app that adds a remote MCP server): add `https://mcp.summerengine.com/mcp`. The app opens the Summer sign-in page. Sign in and choose **Allow**.
- **On your computer** (the `summer-engine` MCP and CLI): run `npx -y summer-engine@latest login`. One browser sign-in covers your Summer account and the store, publishing and Grow tools.

See [MCP setup](/mcp/setup) and [Connect your agent](/publishing/connect-your-agent).

## Troubleshooting

**Can't sign in?**
- Check your email and password
- Ensure your account is verified
- Try resetting your password

**Your agent cannot sign in?**
- On your computer: run `npx -y summer-engine@latest login --force`
- Hosted MCP: remove `https://mcp.summerengine.com/mcp` from your agent, add it again and sign in
