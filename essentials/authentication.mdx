## Creating Your Account

Summer Engine needs an account for hosted AI, asset generation, publishing and your connected agents.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Visit [summerengine.com](https://summerengine.com) and create a free account with Google, GitHub or email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify email">
    If you signed up with email, click the verification link we send you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in through Summer Engine">
    Open Summer Engine and open the chat. When it asks you to sign in, it opens your browser. Sign in there and you return to Summer Engine.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sign in your agent

Your agent signs in with your Summer account in the browser, once. Only the [Summer API](/api/overview), which makes game assets over HTTP, uses a key.

- **Hosted MCP** (Claude, ChatGPT and any app that adds a remote MCP server): add `https://mcp.summerengine.com/mcp`. The app opens the Summer sign-in page. Sign in and choose **Allow**.
- **On your computer** (the `summer-engine` MCP and CLI): run `npx -y summer-engine@latest login`. One browser sign-in covers your Summer account and the store, publishing and Grow tools.
- **Summer API** (any agent or script over HTTP): send `Authorization: Bearer $SUMMER_API_KEY`. Create a key in **Settings > API keys**, or let your agent ask for one and choose **Allow**. See [Keys and limits](/api/keys).

See [MCP setup](/mcp/setup) and [Connect your agent](/publishing/connect-your-agent).

## Troubleshooting

**Can't sign in?**
- Check your email and password
- Ensure your account is verified
- Try resetting your password

**Your agent cannot sign in?**
- On your computer: run `npx -y summer-engine@latest login --force`
- Hosted MCP: remove `https://mcp.summerengine.com/mcp` from your agent, add it again and sign in
