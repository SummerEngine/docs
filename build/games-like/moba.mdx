A MOBA is a long, committed match. Ten players accept a game, split into two
balanced teams and fight over one map full of minions, towers and objectives.
Afterwards the win or loss moves everyone's rank. Summer gives you each of
those pieces. This page shows how they fit together.

## How it fits together

<img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/guide-art/games-like/moba-light.svg" alt="The MOBA loop: players party up, matchmaking forms two balanced teams of five, everyone accepts, the match runs on one server, and the result updates ratings and the leaderboard." />
<img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/guide-art/games-like/moba-dark.svg" alt="The MOBA loop: players party up, matchmaking forms two balanced teams of five, everyone accepts, the match runs on one server, and the result updates ratings and the leaderboard." />

A duo presses Play together. Matchmaking forms two teams of five with the most
even ratings it can find, keeps the duo together, and asks all ten players to
accept. Summer starts a server for the match. Your server runs the map and
decides when a team wins, and every player's rating moves.

## Plan my game

<Prompt description="**Plan my MOBA.** Your agent reads your project and lays out the Summer pieces in the order to build them.">
I'm making a team battle arena like League of Legends in Summer. Read my
project and plan how to build it with these Summer skills, in this order;
if you don't have them installed, find them in the Summer library:

1. `multiplayer` and `multiplayer-project`: the server and client scenes and
   a first Local Play run.
2. `multiplayer-movement`: each player moves their own champion instantly.
3. `multiplayer-state`: minions, towers, gold and abilities, owned by the
   server.
4. `summer-matchmaking`: a ranked 5v5 queue with an accept prompt.
5. `summer-match-results` and `summer-leaderboards`: winners, losers and ranks.
6. `summer-parties` and `summer-world-chat`: queue with friends and talk in
   the match.

For each step, tell me what changes in my project and what I'll see when I
test it. Don't change any files yet.
</Prompt>

## Build the signature features

### Minions, towers and gold

<Prompt description="**Add a server-run lane.** Minion waves, towers and gold that every player sees the same way.">
In my Summer project, add a lane like League of Legends: minion waves that
spawn on a timer, towers that shoot them, and gold for the player who lands
the last hit. Use the Summer `multiplayer-state` skill; if you don't have it
installed, find it in the Summer library.

- The server spawns the minions, runs the towers and awards the gold.
- Every player sees the same minions, health bars and tower state.
- Each player sees only their own gold.

Read my project first and tell me what you'll change. Then test it with Local
Play and tell me what you saw.
</Prompt>

### An accept prompt for ten players

<Prompt description="**Add the match-found prompt.** Ten players accept before the server starts.">
In my Summer project, make my ranked queue a 5v5 with balanced teams, and ask
every player to accept a found match within 20 seconds. Use the Summer
`summer-matchmaking` skill; if you don't have it installed, find it in the
Summer library.

Show a match-found screen with Accept and Decline and a countdown. If the
match doesn't start, send the player back to the menu. Tell me what I can
only check once the game is hosted.
</Prompt>
