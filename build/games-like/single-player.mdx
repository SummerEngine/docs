A single-player game doesn't need a server to feel connected. With Summer,
every player already has an account. Items they unlock or buy belong to that
account, so they survive a reinstall and follow the player to a new device.
Your shop runs through Summer's own checkout, and analytics show you what
players do. This page shows how the pieces fit together.

## How it fits together

<img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/guide-art/games-like/single-player-light.svg" alt="The single-player loop: the player plays on their device, unlocks items owned by their Summer account, buys more in a shop through Summer checkout, and the creator learns from analytics." />
<img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/guide-art/games-like/single-player-dark.svg" alt="The single-player loop: the player plays on their device, unlocks items owned by their Summer account, buys more in a shop through Summer checkout, and the creator learns from analytics." />

The game runs on the player's device, and the player is signed in to Summer.
The items they own come from their account, so your game unlocks content from
what they really own. When they buy something, Summer shows its own checkout
over the game, and your game unlocks the item once Summer confirms it. Your
analytics show which levels players finish and what they buy.

## Plan my game

<Prompt description="**Plan my single-player game.** Your agent reads your project and lays out the Summer pieces in the order to build them.">
I'm making a single-player game in Summer. Read my project and plan how to
add these Summer features with these skills, in this order; if you don't have
them installed, find them in the Summer library:

1. `save-load`: progress saved on the device, with a versioned save format.
2. `in-game-purchases`: a shop that sells items for Sparks through Summer's
   checkout, unlocking only what the player owns.
3. `summer-analytics`: events for the moments I care about, such as a level
   finished or an item bought.
4. `export-and-ship`: get the game ready to publish.

For each step, tell me what changes in my project and what I'll see when I
test it. Don't change any files yet.
</Prompt>

## Build the signature features

### A shop that unlocks for real

<Prompt description="**Add a Sparks shop.** Players buy items through Summer's checkout; the game unlocks what they own.">
In my Summer project, add a shop that sells [ITEMS] for Sparks. Use the
Summer `in-game-purchases` skill; if you don't have it installed, find it in
the Summer library.

- Buying opens Summer's own checkout over the game.
- The game unlocks an item only once the player owns it, so it survives a
  reinstall and works on their other devices.
- If the shop isn't available, hide it and keep the game playable.

Read my project first and tell me where the shop fits. Then tell me what I
can only check once the game is published.
</Prompt>

### Learn what players do

<Prompt description="**Add analytics events.** See where players stop and what they buy.">
In my Summer project, send analytics events for [MOMENTS, for example "level
started, level finished, item bought"]. Use the Summer `summer-analytics`
skill; if you don't have it installed, find it in the Summer library.

Events must never slow down or block the game. Read my project first and tell
me which events you'd add and where, before you change anything.
</Prompt>
