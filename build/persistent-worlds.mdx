Most matches end and are gone. A **persistent World** is a place players come
back to, such as a shared base, a farm or a realm. Your game's server saves it
while people play. When nobody is playing, Summer can let the World sleep, and
the next time someone joins, it wakes up and restores from its last save.

## What your players get

<img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/guide-art/persistent-worlds/lifecycle-light.svg" alt="Four steps for one World: players play and build, the World is saved at checkpoints, it sleeps with no server running while nobody plays, and it wakes up restored from its save when a player returns." />
<img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/guide-art/persistent-worlds/lifecycle-dark.svg" alt="Four steps for one World: players play and build, the World is saved at checkpoints, it sleeps with no server running while nobody plays, and it wakes up restored from its save when a player returns." />

- **The world as they left it.** Walls they built, chests they filled and crops
  they planted are all still there.
- **Their own Worlds, one tap away.** Players find the Worlds they've played
  under **My Worlds** in Summer Games, and your game can offer a "Return to my
  World" button.
- **A warning before planned restarts.** When a World is about to restart or
  sleep for maintenance, your game can tell players in advance, and the World
  saves before it stops.
- **Worlds that survive updates.** Publishing a new version of your game
  doesn't touch existing private Worlds: they keep their saves and the version
  they started on. New Worlds use your newest version.

## What to save where

A World save holds what belongs to the **place**. What belongs to a **player**,
such as their level or the coins in their pocket, goes in
[player progress](/build/player-progress), so it follows them to every World.

<img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/guide-art/persistent-worlds/what-goes-where-light.svg" alt="The World save belongs to the place: walls, chests and crops. Player progress follows the player to every World: level, unlocks, coins and settings." />
<img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/guide-art/persistent-worlds/what-goes-where-dark.svg" alt="The World save belongs to the place: walls, chests and crops. Player progress follows the player to every World: level, unlocks, coins and settings." />

Your game decides what goes in a save, and the save is always one consistent
moment. If a player moves an item from a chest into their inventory, the chest
and the inventory are saved together.

## Pick your setup

### A private World for a group

Each group gets its own World that only its players return to, like a Realm
or a co-op farm. Players come back to it from **My Worlds** or from your
game's menu, and a sleeping World wakes up and restores before they're let in.

<Prompt description="**Make my World persistent.** It saves while people play, sleeps when empty, and players come back to it.">
In my Summer project, turn my World into a persistent World that players come
back to. Use the Summer `summer-world-saves` skill; if you don't have it
installed, find it in the Summer library.

- Save [WHAT BELONGS TO THE PLACE, for example "everything players build and
  the contents of chests"] regularly while people play.
- When the World starts, restore it from its last save before anyone joins.
- Remember each player's World so the menu can offer "Return to my World".
- Keep each player's own progress out of the World save; use the
  `summer-player-data` skill for that.

Read my project first and list what you'd save before you change anything.
Then test it with Local Play and tell me what I can only check once the game
is hosted.
</Prompt>

### Shared Worlds anyone can join

Players who search for a game join an existing World that has a free seat,
waking it up if it's asleep. Summer starts a new World only when none has
room. Good for public servers that grow a community over time.

<img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/guide-art/persistent-worlds/shared-worlds-light.svg" alt="A searching player skips a full World and joins a sleeping World with a free seat, which wakes up. A new World starts only when no existing World has room." />
<img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/guide-art/persistent-worlds/shared-worlds-dark.svg" alt="A searching player skips a full World and joins a sleeping World with a free seat, which wakes up. A new World starts only when no existing World has room." />

<Prompt description="**Add shared Worlds.** Players join an existing World with room before a new one starts.">
In my Summer project, let players join shared persistent Worlds: a player
who presses Play joins an existing World with a free seat, and a new World
starts only when none has room. Use the Summer `summer-world-saves` and
`summer-matchmaking` skills; if you don't have them installed, find them in
the Summer library. The queue should place players in existing Worlds first
(`open_world_first`).

- Each World holds up to [NUMBER] players.
- Save [WHAT BELONGS TO THE PLACE] regularly, and restore it before anyone
  joins.

Read my project first and explain how a player gets from my menu into a
shared World before you change anything.
</Prompt>

### Warn players before a planned restart

Before Summer restarts a World or puts it to sleep for maintenance, your game
gets notice in advance. Use it to warn players and wrap up anything in
progress. Gameplay then pauses for a final save, and the World comes back
exactly as it was.

<img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/guide-art/persistent-worlds/shutdown-light.svg" alt="A timeline: the World gets a notice, the game warns players that the server restarts in 5 minutes, gameplay pauses for a final save, and later the same World comes back restored." />
<img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/guide-art/persistent-worlds/shutdown-dark.svg" alt="A timeline: the World gets a notice, the game warns players that the server restarts in 5 minutes, gameplay pauses for a final save, and later the same World comes back restored." />

<Prompt description="**Warn players before a restart** and make the final save.">
In my Summer project, warn players before my persistent World restarts or
sleeps, and save it before it stops. Use the Summer `summer-world-saves`
skill; if you don't have it installed, find it in the Summer library.

- When a planned stop is announced, show players a countdown, such as
  "Server restarts in 5 minutes", and hide it again if the stop is cancelled.
- Don't start long activities, like a boss fight, close to the stop.
- Make the final save when Summer asks for it.

Keep the regular saves too: an unexpected crash doesn't send a warning.
</Prompt>

## Testing it on your machine

Local Play runs your persistent World with saves kept in memory. Every run
starts with an empty World, saving works and returns a confirmation, and
nothing is kept after the run ends. That's enough to check that your World
saves while people play and restores before anyone joins. Coming back to the
same World after a real restart happens in the hosted game.
