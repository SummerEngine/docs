Sparks are Summer's currency. Players buy Sparks once and spend them in
any Summer game. Your game shows a shop of the items you've published, and the
player presses **Buy**. Summer then asks the player to confirm, takes the
Sparks and adds the item to the player's inventory. Your game never handles
money, prices at checkout or balances, so there's nothing in your code for
anyone to tamper with. You earn 70% of the Sparks players spend in your game,
about 56% of what they pay.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse">
    Your shop lists the items you've published, with their Sparks prices. It
    works from the main menu; players don't need to be in a match.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The player presses Buy, and Summer shows its own purchase screen. The player
    approves or declines there, and Summer takes care of the Sparks, receipts
    and refunds. A player can buy several items together as one purchase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Own it">
    The item appears in the player's inventory, and your game unlocks it. The
    player keeps it after reinstalling, and on every device they play on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You create the items and set their Sparks prices in your game's catalog in
Studio, then publish them. On summer.games, selling items for Sparks is the
**Join the Summer economy** option for games built in Summer. See
[Charge for your game and accept tips](/build/paid-games) for the other ways to
earn.

## Pick your setup

### A shop in your game

Show your published items in your own shop screen, styled like the rest of
your game. Your game asks Summer to check out and shows how the purchase went.
Some purchases take a moment to confirm, so the shop waits for Summer's answer
rather than guessing.

<Prompt description="**Add a shop.** List my items with their Sparks prices and let players buy them.">
In my Summer project, add an in-game shop where players buy my published
items with Sparks. Use the Summer `summer-store` skill; if you don't have it
installed, find it in the Summer library.

- List the items with their names and Sparks prices.
- A Buy button that asks Summer to check out, disabled while a purchase is in
  progress.
- Show the result: bought, cancelled, not enough Sparks, or still confirming.
- Hide the shop wherever it isn't available, and keep the game playable.

Read my project first and fit the shop into my existing menus. Then test it
with Local Play and tell me what I can only check once the game is hosted.
</Prompt>

### Unlock what players own

Players can see everything they own in your game's menus. Their inventory
follows them to every device, so the menus unlock from the inventory, not from
a single purchase. That way an item bought on a phone is also there on the
desktop.

<Prompt description="**Unlock owned items in my menus.** Owned items show as unlocked everywhere.">
In my Summer project, unlock items in my menus from what the player owns. Use
the Summer `summer-store` skill; if you don't have it installed, find it in the
Summer library.

- Show which of my items the player owns, and update when that changes.
- While the inventory is still loading, show it as loading, not as empty.
- Items the player doesn't own link to the shop.

Read my project first and tell me which menus need to change before you
change anything. Then test it with Local Play and tell me what you saw.
</Prompt>

### Items that change the match

When an item changes what happens in a match, such as a skin other players
see or a character a player picks, your game's server checks the player's
inventory itself before it accepts. The player's game asking for the item
doesn't count as proof, so nobody can wear something they haven't bought.

<img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/build/store/check-dark.svg" alt="A player's game asks to wear the gold crown. The game's server reads that player's inventory itself: if the crown is owned, everyone sees it; if not, the request is refused." />

<Prompt description="**Check ownership on my server.** Players can only use items they own, and everyone sees them.">
In my Summer project, let players use [ITEM, for example "skins"] in a match,
checked by my server. Use the Summer `summer-store` skill; if you don't have it
installed, find it in the Summer library.

- The player picks an item they own; the server reads that player's inventory
  and accepts or refuses.
- Every player in the match sees the accepted item, including players who
  join later.

Read my project first and explain where the check fits in my server before you
change anything. Then test it with Local Play and tell me what you saw.
</Prompt>

## Testing it on your machine

Under Local Play the shop and checkout report that they aren't available, so
you test the path where your game hides the shop. Owned items work: give your
test players items in your Local Play test users file, and your menus and your
server's ownership check see them. Real purchases need a hosted game.
